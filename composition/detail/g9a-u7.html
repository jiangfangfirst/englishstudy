定稿范文

Teenagers Should Be Allowed to Choose

I think teenagers should be allowed to make some of their own choices.

For example, we should be allowed to choose our own clothes, because we are old enough to know what we like. We should also be allowed to choose our own hobbies. Some parents don't allow their children to play computer games at all, but I think playing games in moderation is a good way to relax.

However, I also agree that some rules are necessary. We should not be allowed to use mobile phones during class, because the noise affects not only our own study but also others'. Besides, we should not be allowed to stay out too late at night, because it may be dangerous for teenagers.

In short, teenagers need freedom, but we also need rules to guide us. The key is to find a balance between freedom and rules.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

我认为青少年应该被允许做一些自己的选择。

例如，我们应该被允许选择自己的衣服，因为我们已经足够大，知道自己喜欢什么。我们也应该被允许选择自己的爱好。有些父母完全不允许孩子玩电脑游戏，但我认为适度玩游戏是放松的好方式。

然而，我也同意有些规则是必要的。我们不应该被允许上课使用手机，因为噪音不仅影响自己学习，也影响他人。此外，我们不应该被允许晚上在外面待到太晚，因为这对青少年可能很危险。

总之，青少年需要自由，但也需要规则引导。关键是在自由和规则之间找到平衡。

范文亮点

  1. 用 because 合并选衣服与足够大两句。
  2. 把 OK 改为 a good way to relax，更书面。
  3. 用 not only…but also… 扩展手机影响。
  4. 加 at night 与 for teenagers，使原因更完整。
