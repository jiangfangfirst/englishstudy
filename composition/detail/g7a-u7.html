定稿范文

Happy Birthday!

I'm Li Ming. I'm 13 years old, and my birthday is on 26th October.

On my birthday, my parents usually hold a party for me. My mother makes a big birthday cake with fruit on it. My friends come to my home and give me nice presents. We sing songs, play games and eat delicious food together.

Before blowing out the candles, I make a wish. I always have a wonderful time on my birthday, and I love my birthday very much.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

我是李明，今年13岁，生日在10月26日。

生日那天，父母通常会为我举办派对。妈妈做一个带水果的大生日蛋糕。朋友们来我家，送我漂亮的礼物。我们一起唱歌、玩游戏、吃美食。

吹蜡烛前，我许了一个愿。我生日总是过得很开心，我非常喜欢我的生日。

KP

考点

一般现在时日期表达 on 26th Octoberbefore + 动名词频率副词 usually / always
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 用 with fruit on it 补充蛋糕细节。
  2. 用 nice 修饰 presents，更有温度。
  3. 把 before 短语前置，使句式有变化。
  4. 结尾用 wonderful 替换 good，并加 and I love… 升华情感。
