定稿范文

Traditional Festivals

The Spring Festival is one of the most important traditional Chinese festivals. It is usually celebrated in January or February, and people across the country celebrate it in many different ways.

Before the festival, families are busy buying new clothes and preparing lots of delicious food. On New Year's Eve, all the family members get together and have a big dinner. On this day, people in the north of China usually eat dumplings, while people in the south are used to eating tangyuan.

After the big dinner, some people like watching the Spring Festival Gala, which they think is really fantastic. Others enjoy going out to watch the colorful fireworks to welcome the coming new year.

During the festival, people always visit their relatives and say "Happy New Year" to each other. Besides, children can wear their new clothes and get red packets that are filled with lucky money. That is why they all love this festival so much. It is also my favourite festival because it always makes me feel happy and excited.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

春节是中国最重要的传统节日之一。它通常在1月或2月庆祝，全国各地的人以多种方式庆祝。

春节前，家家户户忙着买新衣服、准备很多美食。除夕夜，全家人聚在一起吃年夜饭。这一天，北方人通常吃饺子，而南方人习惯吃汤圆。

年夜饭后，有些人喜欢看春晚，他们觉得非常精彩。其他人喜欢出去看五彩缤纷的烟花迎接新年。

春节期间，人们总是走亲访友，互相说“新年快乐”。此外，孩子们可以穿新衣，拿到装满压岁钱的红包。这就是他们如此喜爱这个节日的原因。它也是我最喜欢的节日，因为它总让我感到快乐和兴奋。

范文亮点

  1. 加 most important 点明春节地位。
  2. 用 while 连接南北对比。
  3. 把看春晚两句合并为 which 定语从句，补上原文缺失的定语从句考点。
  4. 用 be filled with lucky money 替换啰嗦表达；用 That is why… 收束因果。
