定稿范文

Could You Please Tell Me?

Dear Sir or Madam,

My name is He Wei and I'm from Beijing, China. I'll be coming to your school soon for a short study vacation, and I will leave for your school on July 10th.

First, I'd like to know what course I will study in your school, and I also wonder when the course will start. Besides, I wonder where I will live during my study vacation, such as a school dormitory or a local family. Would you mind telling me what food is served in your school and where I can have meals?

I enjoy sports and reading as well. Could you please tell me what after-school activities I can take part in during my stay? Finally, I wonder how I can get to your school from the airport when I arrive.

Thank you so much for your help, and I'm looking forward to your reply.

Yours,

He Wei

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

尊敬的先生/女士：

我叫何伟，来自中国北京。我很快要来贵校参加短期学习，7月10日出发。

首先，我想知道将在贵校学习什么课程，也想知道课程何时开始。此外，我想知道学习期间住在哪里，比如学校宿舍还是当地家庭。您介意告诉我学校提供什么食物、在哪里用餐吗？

我也喜欢运动和阅读。您能告诉我学习期间可以参加哪些课外活动吗？最后，我想知道到达后如何从机场到贵校。

非常感谢您的帮助，期待您的回复。

你的，

何伟

KP

考点

宾语从句陈述语序Would you mind doing…?look forward to doingI wonder…
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 用 First / Besides / Finally 串联问题，条理清楚。
  2. 交替使用 I'd like to know / I wonder / Would you mind / Could you please，避免重复。
  3. 把住宿具体化为 dormitory or local family。
  4. 补 from the airport 使问题更具体。
