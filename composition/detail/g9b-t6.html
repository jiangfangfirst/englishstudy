定稿范文

Sports and Health

The athlete I admire most is Liu Xiang, the first Asian to win an Olympic gold medal in the men's 110-metre hurdles at the 2004 Athens Olympics.

What makes him so admirable is not just his achievements, but also his strong spirit. He trained hard every day, pushing himself to the limit. Even when he faced injuries and setbacks, he never thought of giving up. The more difficult the training was, the stronger he became.

Liu Xiang's story tells us that success comes from sweat and perseverance. It is his courage and determination that inspire me to face challenges bravely in my own life. Whenever I feel like giving up, I think of him and keep going. I hope that one day I can be as strong as he is.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

我最敬佩的运动员是刘翔——2004年雅典奥运会男子110米栏金牌得主，第一位获此殊荣的亚洲人。

让他令人敬佩的不仅是成就，还有他坚强的精神。他每天刻苦训练，把自己逼到极限。即使面对伤病和挫折，也从未想过放弃。训练越艰难，他就越强大。

刘翔的故事告诉我们，成功来自汗水和毅力。正是他的勇气和决心激励我勇敢面对生活中的挑战。每当我想要放弃时，就会想起他，继续前行。我希望有一天能像他一样坚强。

KP

考点

what 主语从句强调句 It is…that…the + 比较级Even when 让步从句whenever 时间状语从句
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 补 2004 年雅典奥运会，使事实更扎实。
  2. 加 strong 修饰 spirit，与下文呼应。
  3. 用 tells 替换 teaches，避免与 inspire 重复。
  4. 加 bravely 使强调句更有力；补 that 使宾语从句更规范。
