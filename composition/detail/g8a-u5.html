定稿范文

What a Delicious Meal!

Today I want to share a delicious dish with you — tomato and egg noodles. It's easy to make, and it only takes a few minutes.

First, wash the tomatoes and cut them into pieces. Then break two eggs into a bowl and mix them well. Next, heat some oil in a pan and fry the eggs until they are golden. After that, add the tomatoes and stir-fry for about two minutes. Then pour in some water and bring it to a boil. Finally, put the noodles into the boiling water and cook for about five minutes.

Add some salt, and your tomato and egg noodles are ready! It's a simple but delicious meal. I hope you can try it at home!

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

今天我想和大家分享一道美食——番茄鸡蛋面。它很容易做，只需几分钟。

首先，把番茄洗净切块。然后打两个鸡蛋到碗里搅拌均匀。接着，在锅里热油，把鸡蛋煎至金黄。之后加入番茄翻炒约两分钟。然后倒入水烧开。最后把面条放入沸水中煮约五分钟。

加点盐，你的番茄鸡蛋面就做好了！这是一道简单又美味的饭。希望你能在家试试！

KP

考点

祈使句顺序词 First / Then / Next / After that / Finallyuntil 时间状语从句
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 加 and it only takes a few minutes，呼应易做。
  2. 把 you're done 改为点题的 are ready，并重现菜名。
  3. 补 at home 使邀请更具体。
  4. 祈使句语气统一，顺序词完整。
