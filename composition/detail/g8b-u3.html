定稿范文

Growing Up

Dear Mr. Wang,

I'm writing to express my sincere thanks to you.

Last month, I had a really difficult time with my English study. I almost gave up because I could not understand the grammar rules at all. However, you were so patient with me. You spent extra time after class explaining the rules again and again until I finally understood them. You also encouraged me not to give up and told me to be confident.

With your help, I have made great progress in the final exam. What you have done means a lot to me. I will always remember your kindness and your encouragement. Thank you again from the bottom of my heart.

Yours sincerely,

Li Hua

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

亲爱的王老师：

我写信是为了向您表达我诚挚的感谢。

上个月，我在英语学习上遇到了很大困难。因为完全不懂语法规则，我差点放弃。然而，您对我非常耐心。您课后花额外时间一遍又一遍地讲解，直到我终于弄懂。您还鼓励我不要放弃，告诉我要自信。

在您的帮助下，我在期末考试中取得了很大进步。您所做的一切对我意义重大。我会永远记住您的好意和鼓励。再次发自内心地感谢您。

您真诚的，

李华

KP

考点

感谢信格式现在完成时 have madeWhat 引导主语从句with one's help
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 加 really 强化困难程度。
  2. 补 rules 与 at all，具体点明困难。
  3. 加 finally 体现弄懂过程。
  4. 补 told me to be confident 丰富细节；用 have made 紧扣考点；结尾并列 kindness 与 encouragement。
