定稿范文

Growth and Change

My life has changed a lot in the last few years.

I used to be short and thin, but now I am tall and strong. I used to be afraid of speaking in public, and in the past I didn't even dare to speak in front of the class, but now I am more outgoing and confident.

As for my study, I used to be weak in English, but with my English teacher's help, I have made great progress in it. In my free time, I used to play computer games a lot, but now I prefer reading books and playing sports.

These changes have not only made me healthier but also helped me become a better person. I believe that as long as we keep working hard, we will become better and better.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

在过去的几年里，我的生活变化很大。

我过去又矮又瘦，现在又高又壮。我过去害怕在公共场合讲话，甚至不敢在全班面前发言，但现在更外向自信。

至于学习，我过去英语很差，但在英语老师的帮助下，我取得了很大进步。空闲时，我过去常玩电脑游戏，现在更喜欢读书和运动。

这些变化不仅让我更健康，也帮我成为更好的人。我相信只要继续努力，就会越来越好。

KP

考点

used to dobut now 对比现在完成时not only…but also…as long as 条件句
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 补不敢在全班面前发言的具体场景，打破四组排比雷同。
  2. 加 English 使指代明确；用 in it 补进步范围。
  3. 把 doing sports 改为 playing sports 更自然。
  4. 用 not only…but also… 概括多方面变化。
