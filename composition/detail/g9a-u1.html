定稿范文

The Changing World

I used to live in a small town in the south of China. The streets were narrow and crowded, and there were only a few small shops along them. People used to ride bicycles or walk to get around. The river behind our house was so clean that we could see fish swimming in it, and we children often played by it after school. Life was simple and peaceful.

But now, great changes have taken place. The government has built wide new roads and a modern railway station. Many tall buildings have taken the place of the old houses. More people have moved here from other places, and the town has become much busier. A new shopping mall has opened, and young people who used to work in big cities have returned to start their own businesses.

However, these changes have brought some problems as well. The river is no longer clean because of the waste from the new factories. The air is not as fresh as it used to be. Traffic jams have become common during rush hours.

In my opinion, development is necessary, but we should also protect the environment. Only by balancing progress with nature can our hometown become a better place for everyone.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

我过去住在中国南方的一个小镇。街道又窄又挤，沿街只有几家小商店。人们过去骑自行车或步行出行。我们家后面的河很清澈，可以看到鱼儿游来游去，我们孩子放学后常在河边玩。生活简单而宁静。

但现在，巨大变化已经发生。政府建了宽阔的新路和现代化火车站。许多高楼取代了旧房子。更多人从外地搬来，小镇变得繁忙多了。一家新商场开业了，过去在大城市工作的年轻人也回来创业了。

然而，这些变化也带来了一些问题。由于新工厂的废物，河水不再清澈。空气也不像过去那么新鲜。交通拥堵在高峰期变得很常见。

在我看来，发展是必要的，但我们也应该保护环境。只有平衡进步与自然，我们的家乡才能成为每个人更好的地方。

范文亮点

  1. 加 crowded 与 along them，使旧街道画面更具体。
  2. 补童年河边玩耍细节，与后文河水变脏形成情感对照。
  3. 用 wide / modern 使发展具象化。
  4. 补 the waste 冠词更规范。
