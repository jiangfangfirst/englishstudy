定稿范文

Making a Difference

Dear Mr. Smith,

I am writing to apply to work as a volunteer at the animal shelter.

My name is Li Hua and I am a middle school student. I love animals very much, and I really want to do something for them. I have a pet dog at home, so I know how to look after animals patiently. I am free on weekends, and I can help feed the animals, clean their cages and walk the dogs in the nearby park. I am also good at taking photos. I can take nice photos of the animals and help them find warm new homes.

I believe I can make a difference to their lives. I hope you can give me this chance. Thank you very much!

Yours,

Li Hua

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

亲爱的Smith先生：

我写信申请在动物收容所做志愿者。

我叫李华，是一名中学生。我非常喜欢动物，真心想为它们做点什么。我家养了一只宠物狗，所以我知道如何耐心照顾动物。我周末有空，可以帮忙喂动物、清理笼子、在附近公园遛狗。我还擅长拍照，可以给动物拍好看的照片，帮它们找到温暖的新家。

我相信我能对它们的生活带来改变。希望您能给我这个机会。非常感谢！

你的，

李华

KP

考点

申请信格式be good at doingbe free on weekendsso 因果help sb. do
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 用 work as a volunteer 明确志愿身份。
  2. 加 really 强化动机。
  3. 用 look after 替换 take care 避免重复。
  4. 补 in the nearby park 使遛狗更具体；把 make a difference 落到 their lives。
