定稿范文

My School

Dear Lucy,

Thanks for your email and your interest in Chinese school life. I'm Li Hua from Wusi Middle School.

Our school is big and beautiful, with many modern facilities. We have bright classrooms, a large library full of books, a small gym for sports, and a playground where we can relax and chat during breaks.

My favorite place is the library. I love reading, and the library has so many interesting books about history and science. It is quiet and sunny, so it is a great place to learn and discover new things.

What's your favorite part of your school? Hope to hear from you soon!

Best,

Li Hua

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

亲爱的Lucy：

感谢你的邮件，也谢谢你对中国学校生活的兴趣。我是五四中学的李华。

我们学校又大又美，有很多现代化设施。我们有明亮的教室、一个装满书的图书馆、一个小型体育馆，还有一个课间可以放松聊天的操场。

我最喜欢的地方是图书馆。我喜欢阅读，图书馆里有很多关于历史和科学的有趣书籍。那里安静又明亮，是学习和探索新事物的好地方。

你最喜欢你们学校的哪个部分？期待你的回信！

祝好，

李华

KP

考点

邮件格式where 定语从句with 复合结构full of
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 用 full of 替换 filled with，避免分词越级。
  2. 用 where 从句中的 relax and chat 补充课间活动。
  3. 补充图书馆环境（quiet and sunny）使喜欢有依据。
