定稿范文

It Must Belong to Carla

This morning, I found a strange backpack on a desk in our classroom. Whose backpack is it? It must belong to someone in our class. Let me guess.

It can't be Lily's because she is at home today. It might be Tom's because he always carries a big backpack. But wait — when I opened it, I found a pink notebook and some hair clips inside. It must be a girl's backpack. Could it be Anna's? She likes pink.

Then I noticed a name tag on the backpack, and it said "Carla". So it must belong to Carla! I will give it back to her after class.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

今天早上，我在教室的一张桌子上发现一个奇怪的书包。这是谁的书包？一定是班里某个人的。让我猜猜。

不可能是Lily的，因为她今天在家。可能是Tom的，因为他总背一个大书包。但等等——当我打开它时，发现里面有一个粉色笔记本和几个发夹。这一定是女生的书包。会是Anna的吗？她喜欢粉色。

然后我注意到书包上有个名牌，上面写着“Carla”。所以它一定是Carla的！下课后我会还给她。

KP

考点

情态动词表推测 must / can't / might / couldwhose 特殊疑问句when 时间状语从句belong to
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 补 This morning 与 on a desk 增强现场感。
  2. 用 when 从句补打开书包动作，使线索自然。
  3. 用 Then 承接推理节奏。
  4. 用 noticed 替换 I see，更自然；保留 must 表最终确认。
