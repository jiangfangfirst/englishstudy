定稿范文

You and Me

Hello! My name is Zhang Wei and I'm 13 years old. I live in Guangzhou, a big city in the south of China, with my mom and dad. I'm in Class 4, Grade 7 at Guangzhou No. 2 Middle School.

I'm a friendly and outgoing boy who loves to meet new people. After class, I like reading adventure books and playing soccer with my friends on the playground.

I think it would be great to have more friends to share exciting stories and have fun together. If you also love sports or reading, please write to me! Do you want to be my friend?

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

你好！我叫张伟，今年13岁，和爸爸妈妈住在广州——中国南方的一座大城市。我在广州第二中学七年级四班。

我是一个友好外向的男孩，喜欢结识新朋友。课后，我喜欢读冒险故事书，和朋友们在操场上踢足球。

我觉得能有更多朋友一起分享精彩故事、一起玩耍会很棒。如果你也喜欢运动或阅读，请给我写信！你想成为我的朋友吗？

KP

考点

一般现在时who 引导定语从句if 条件句I think it would be great to…
STAR

范文亮点

  1. 用同位语补充城市信息。
  2. after class 与 on the playground 补充时间地点细节。
  3. 结尾用 if 条件句发出具体邀请，互动性强。
