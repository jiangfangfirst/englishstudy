定稿范文

Earth First

Our environment is facing serious problems today. There is more and more pollution around us. Air pollution, water pollution and noise pollution have caused many serious problems to our health and our daily life.

It is high time that we took action to protect our environment. What can we do in our daily life?

First, we can save water and electricity. For example, we should turn off the lights when we leave the classroom. Second, we should reduce the use of plastic bags and use reusable ones instead. Third, we can plant more trees and take part in environmental activities in our community.

As the saying goes, "Many hands make light work." If everyone makes a small effort, we can make a big difference. Let's work together to make our world greener and cleaner.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

如今我们的环境正面临严重问题。周围的污染越来越多。空气污染、水污染和噪音污染已经给我们的健康和日常生活造成了许多严重问题。

是时候我们采取行动保护环境了。在日常生活中我们能做什么呢？

首先，我们可以节约用水用电。例如，离开教室时应该关灯。其次，我们应该减少塑料袋的使用，改用可重复使用的袋子。第三，我们可以多种树，参加社区的环保活动。

俗话说，“人多好办事。”如果每个人都付出一点努力，我们就能带来巨大改变。让我们共同努力，让世界更绿更干净。
