定稿范文

What Are the Shirts Made Of?

My favorite product is a beautiful silk scarf from my mother. It is made of soft silk, which comes from silkworm cocoons. Silk is produced in many places in China, especially in the south.

The scarf is made by hand. First, workers collect silk threads from silkworm cocoons. Then they weave the threads into cloth. After that, they dye the cloth with natural colors taken from flowers and plants, and print beautiful patterns on it. Finally, the cloth is cut and sewn into a scarf.

The scarf feels soft and smooth against my skin. It is not only beautiful but also warm in winter. In China, silk scarves are often given as gifts to friends and family. They are a symbol of Chinese culture.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

我最喜欢的产品是妈妈送我的一条漂亮丝巾。它由柔软的丝绸制成，丝绸来自蚕茧。丝绸在中国许多地方都有生产，尤其是南方。

丝巾是手工制作的。首先，工人从蚕茧中收集丝线。然后他们把丝线织成布。之后用从花草中提取的天然染料给布染色，并在上面印上美丽的图案。最后，布被裁剪缝制成丝巾。

丝巾贴在皮肤上柔软光滑。它不仅漂亮，冬天还很保暖。在中国，丝巾常作为礼物送给亲友，是中国文化的象征。
