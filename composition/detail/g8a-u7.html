定稿范文

Be Wise with Money

As teenagers, we should learn to be wise with money. Here is some advice for you.

First, we should make a plan for our pocket money. We can divide it into different parts, such as food, books and savings. Second, we should not spend too much money on unnecessary things, like expensive toys or games. Instead, we'd better spend our money on what we really need. Third, it's a good idea to save some money for the future. We can put it in a bank or a money box.

Besides, we can learn to earn money by doing some part-time jobs when we are old enough. In a word, being wise with money means spending it properly and saving it for the future.

CN

中文翻译

中文对照

作为青少年，我们应该学会合理理财。这里有一些建议。

首先，我们应该为零花钱制定计划，把它分成不同部分，比如食物、书籍和储蓄。其次，我们不应该在不需要的东西上花太多钱，比如昂贵的玩具或游戏。相反，我们最好把钱花在真正需要的地方。第三，为未来存一些钱是个好主意。我们可以把它存进银行或存钱罐。

此外，等我们足够大时，可以学着做兼职来赚钱。总之，合理理财意味着正确花钱并为未来储蓄。
