定稿范文

Stay Healthy

Nowadays, more and more people have come to focus on teenagers' health. As we know, health is the most important thing for everyone. Here is some advice on how to keep healthy.

First of all, we should develop good eating habits. We must eat more fresh fruit and vegetables and less junk food, because proper food gives us energy for the whole day. Secondly, doing exercise every day is necessary for us. We can run in the morning, swim at weekends or play ball games with our friends after school. Thirdly, we should get enough sleep. As the saying goes, "Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy and wise."

Besides physical health, mental health is also important. When we feel stressed or unhappy, we can talk to our parents or teachers. We can also listen to soft music or do something we enjoy to relax ourselves.

In a word, a healthy life means both a healthy body and a healthy mind. If we follow this advice, we will feel energetic and enjoy our school life.

中文翻译

中文对照

如今，越来越多的人开始关注青少年的健康。众所周知，健康对每个人来说都是最重要的。以下是一些保持健康的建议。

首先，我们应该养成良好的饮食习惯。必须多吃新鲜水果和蔬菜，少吃垃圾食品，因为适当的食物给我们提供一整天的能量。其次，每天锻炼对我们很有必要。我们可以早上跑步、周末游泳或放学后和朋友打球。第三，我们应该获得充足睡眠。俗话说，“早睡早起使人健康、富有和聪明。”

除了身体健康，心理健康也很重要。当我们感到压力或不开心时，可以和父母或老师谈谈。也可以听轻音乐或做喜欢的事来放松自己。

总之，健康的生活意味着健康的身体和健康的心灵。如果遵循这些建议，我们会感到精力充沛，享受校园生活。
