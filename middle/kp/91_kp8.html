语法核心

本单元两大语法
主谓一致（一）：不定代词与并列主语

everybody/everyone/each/neither of/either of 等不定代词作主语，谓语用单数：Everybody is ready...。

both...and... / both of 连接两个主语，谓语用复数：Both kung fu and boxing are popular。

Everyone in my group is ready for Sports Day.
Both kung fu and boxing are popular.
主谓一致（二）：就近与就远原则

either...or... / neither...nor... / not only...but also... / there be 遵循就近原则：Either you or I am going to attend...。

with / together with / as well as / except / besides 引出插入语遵循就远原则：The coach with two players is at the gate。

Either you or I am going to attend the opening ceremony.
The captain, together with two players, is at the gate now.
主谓一致（三）：特殊主语

动名词短语作主语视为单数：Playing sports makes us strong。

the news、maths、时间/距离/金钱整体作单数：Ten years is a long time。

family/team/group/class 强调整体用单数、强调成员用复数；the old、police 表示一类人谓语用复数；a number of 用复数、the number of 用单数。

Playing sports makes us strong.
Ten years is a long time for everyone.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
不定代词作主语用单数

everybody/everyone/each/neither of/either of 作主语，谓语用单数。

Everybody has a chance to play.
2
both...and... 用复数

both A and B / both of 作主语，谓语用复数。

Both kung fu and boxing are popular sports among young people.
3
就近原则：either...or / neither...nor / not only...but also

谓语与离它最近的主语一致。

Either you or he is going to take part.
4
就近原则：there be

there be 中 be 由最近的主语决定。

There is a pen and some books on the desk.
5
就远原则：with / as well as / together with

真正主语在前，插入语不影响谓语。

The teacher with his students is going to visit the museum.
6
动名词短语作主语用单数

动名词作主语视为单数。

Exercising every day makes us stronger.
7
形复实单：news / maths

the news、maths 形式复数意义单数，谓语用单数。

The news about the games is exciting.
8
时间/距离/金钱整体作单数

Ten years / Fifteen seconds / Five hundred dollars 视为整体，谓语用单数。

Ten kilometres is not a long distance.
9
family/team/class 主谓一致

强调整体用单数，强调成员用复数。

My family are watching TV now.
His family is a big one. They are all very kind.
10
the + 形容词表一类人 / police

the old、the young、police 等谓语用复数。

The police are looking for the lost child.
11
a number of / the number of

a number of 许多（+复数名词，谓语复数）；the number of ……的数量（谓语单数）。

A number of students are playing on the playground.
12
情景交际——祝贺与鼓励

Congratulations! 祝贺；Well done! / Good luck! / Come on! 鼓励。

—I won the 100-metre race! —Congratulations!

语法易错点总结

考前必看
!
姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. everybody/everyone/each 作主语谓语用单数。
  2. both...and... 谓语用复数；either...or... / neither...nor... 就近原则。
  3. with/together with/as well as/except 引出插入语，谓语看真正主语（就远原则）。
  4. 动名词作主语谓语用单数。
  5. the news、maths 形复实单，谓语用单数。
  6. 时间、距离、金钱作整体时谓语用单数：Ten years is...。
  7. family/team/class 强调整体单数、强调成员复数。
  8. the old/the young/police 表一类人谓语用复数。
  9. a number of 谓语复数；the number of 谓语单数。
  10. motto 复数 mottoes/mottos；take pride in（名词）/ be proud of（形容词）。
  11. try out for 参加选拔，sign up for 报名参加，介词 for 不可省。
  12. take the lead 领先；catch up with 追上；make up ground on 缩小差距，三者对象不同。
