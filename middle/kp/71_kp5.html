语法核心

本单元两大语法
情态动词 can

can 表能力，后接动词原形；否定 can't；疑问 Can + 主语 + 动词原形?；无人称和数的变化。

can 与 be able to 同义，但 be able to 有人称时态变化。

The fish can swim, but it can't fly.
—Can you play the guitar? —Yes, I can.
—Can you play the piano? —No, I can't. But I can play the violin.
play the + 乐器 / play + 球类

乐器前加 the：play the piano / the violin / the drums；球类前不加 the：play football / basketball / chess。

She can play the violin very well.
My brother is good at playing basketball.
be good at / be interested in

be good at 擅长（后接名词/动名词）· be interested in 对……感兴趣 · be good with 善于与……相处。

She is good at drawing. She draws well.
I am very interested in the story. It's so funny.

重点词汇精讲

1–11
club/klʌb/n. 俱乐部；社团

join the art club 加入美术社团。

—Which club do you want to join? —The chess club.
join/dʒɔɪn/v. 加入

join + 组织/团体/活动。

I want to join the music club. I like music.
常考join 后直接接组织，不用 join in 接组织。
can/kæn/modal v. 能；会

后接动词原形；can't 否定。

He can sing and dance.
swim/swɪm/v. 游泳

swimming 动名词；go swimming 去游泳。

She can't swim, but she can dance well.
paint/peɪnt/v. 绘画；涂颜料

painting n. 绘画；画。

My sister is good at painting. She can draw beautiful pictures.
draw/drɔː/v. 画；绘制

draw a picture 画画。

She is good at drawing. She draws well.
dance/dɑːns/v. 跳舞 · n. 舞蹈

dancer 舞者。

My brother can dance. He wants to dance after school.
sing/sɪŋ/v. 唱；唱歌

singer 歌手。

—Can he sing? —Yes, he can. He can sing well.
chess/tʃes/n. 国际象棋

play chess 下棋（不加 the）。

—What can you do? —I can play chess.
易错play chess 不加 the，与 play the piano 不同。
drum/drʌm/n. 鼓

play the drums 打鼓（乐器加 the）。

My brother can play the drums in the school band.
violin/ˌvaɪəˈlɪn/n. 小提琴

play the violin 拉小提琴。

—What can your sister do? —She can play the violin.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
考点一：can 后接动词原形

can / can't 后必须接动词原形，无人称变化：He can sing.（不是 He can sings.）

Linda can speak three languages.
2
考点二：can 的疑问与回答

Can + 主语 + 动词原形? 回答 Yes, 主语 + can. / No, 主语 + can't.

—Can your sister play the piano? —Yes, she can.
3
考点三：play 与冠词

乐器前加 the：play the piano；球类/棋类前不加 the：play chess / play football。

—Can you play the guitar or the violin? —I can play the violin, but I can't play the guitar.
4
考点四：join 的用法

join 加入组织/团体/活动：join the club；join sb. 加入某人行列。

—I want to join the swimming club. —Sounds good.
5
考点五：be good at 后接 doing

be good at 后接名词或动名词。

My brother is good at dancing. He wants to join the dance club.
6
考点六：be interested in 后接 doing

be interested in 对……感兴趣，in 后接名词/动名词。

She is interested in collecting stamps, so she joins the stamp club.
7
考点七：be good at 与 be good with

be good at 擅长；be good with 善于与……相处（接人或物）。

Mrs. Gao is good at music. She can be good with children in the music club.
8
考点八：act out 表演

act out 表演、演出（剧本/故事）。

—What do you do in the drama club? —We often act out stories.
9
考点九：want to do / would like to do

want to do sth. 想要做某事；would like to do 愿意做某事。

—What club would you like to join? —I can sing and dance, so I want to be in the music club.
10
考点十：询问能力的情景交际

What can you do? 询问能力；Can you...? 询问是否具备某能力。

—What can you do? —I can play chess.
11
考点十一：sing / swim 等动词三单

一般现在时主语三单时动词变化：She sings well.

She joins the art club. She draws pictures there every week.
12
考点十二：why 询问加入原因

Why do you want to join the club? 用 Because... 回答。

—Why do you want to join the dance club? —Because I like dancing very much.

语法易错点总结

姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. can / can't 后接动词原形，无人称和数的变化。
  2. 乐器前加 the：play the piano；球类/棋类不加 the：play chess。
  3. join 后直接接组织：join the club，不能说 join in the club。
  4. be good at 后接名词或动名词：be good at drawing。
  5. be interested in 对……感兴趣，in 后接 doing。
  6. be good with 善于与……相处，be good at 擅长。
  7. Can you...? 回答 Yes, I can. / No, I can't.。
  8. act out 表演；演出。
  9. want to do sth. 想要做某事。
  10. Why do you want to join...? 用 Because 回答。
  11. go swimming 去游泳，go + doing 表活动。
  12. let's 后接动词原形：Let's go.。
