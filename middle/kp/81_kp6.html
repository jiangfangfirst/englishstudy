本 KP 三个关键：① be going to 后接动词原形；② there be 将来时用 there is/are going to be，不能用 have；③ be going to 强调计划，will 强调临时决定。

语法核心

本单元两大语法
语法一：be going to 结构

be going to do sth. 表示"打算/计划做某事"，be 动词随主语变化（I am / he is / they are），to 后接动词原形。否定在 be 后加 not；疑问把 be 提到主语前。

there be 将来时：there is/are going to be 或 there will be，不能与 have 连用，be 与最近主语一致。现在进行时表将来：表示按计划即将发生的动作。

—What's your plan for the weekend, Helen? —I am going to visit my grandparents.
——海伦，你周末的计划是什么？——我打算去看望我的祖父母。
There is going to be a talent show in our school tomorrow.
明天我们学校将有一场才艺表演。
—It will be a sunny day tomorrow. Let's climb the Zijin Mountain with Alice! —Sounds great! But she is going to leave for Suzhou soon.
——明天会是晴天。我们和爱丽丝一起去爬紫金山吧！——听起来不错！但她很快就要去苏州了。
语法二：will 表示将来

will + 动词原形，常与 tomorrow、next week、in the future 连用。be going to 强调计划/已有安排；will 强调单纯将来或临时决定。

Last term I failed my English test, but I will work harder this term.
上学期我英语考试不及格，但这一学期我会更加努力。
Next weekend, students will put on plays to celebrate the New Year.
下周末，学生们将上演戏剧来庆祝新年。
—As your closest friend, I will try every possible way to help you with the work. —You are so kind.
——作为你最亲密的朋友，我会尽一切可能帮助你完成这项工作。——你真好。

重点词汇精讲

1–20
resolution/ˌrezəˈluːʃn/n. 决心；决议

搭配 make a resolution 下决心；New Year's resolution 新年决心。

I made a New Year's resolution to learn a foreign language this year.
我今年下了新年决心要学一门外语。
relationship/rɪˈleɪʃnʃɪp/n. 关系；联系

搭配 have a close relationship with sb.。

—I hear you have a close relationship with Mrs. Miller. —Yes. Whenever I go to Paris on business, I will go to visit her.
——我听说你和米勒夫人关系很好。——是的。每次我去巴黎出差，我都会去看望她。
literature/ˈlɪtrətʃə(r)/n. 文学
As a great writer, Mark Twain has an important place in the field of literature.
作为一位伟大的作家，马克·吐温在文学领域占有重要地位。
pilot/ˈpaɪlət/n. 飞行员
My cousin is going to be a pilot, so he is taking flying lessons now.
我表哥打算当飞行员，所以他现在在上飞行课。
engineer/ˌendʒɪˈnɪə(r)/n. 工程师
He is an engineer; he builds many tall buildings and bridges in our city.
他是一名工程师；他在我们城市建造了许多高楼和桥梁。
designer/dɪˈzaɪnə(r)/n. 设计师
—What do you want to be in the future? —I want to be a fashion designer, and I can design all kinds of beautiful dresses.
——你将来想做什么？——我想成为一名时装设计师，我可以设计各种漂亮的裙子。
violinist/ˌvaɪəˈlɪnɪst/n. 小提琴手
She practices the violin every day and wants to be a violinist.
她每天练习小提琴，想成为一名小提琴手。
pianist/ˈpiənɪst/n. 钢琴家
He plays the piano very well and wants to be a pianist.
他钢琴弹得很好，想成为一名钢琴家。
scientist/ˈsaɪəntɪst/n. 科学家
Her dream is to be a scientist and invent new things for people.
她的梦想是成为一名科学家，为人们发明新东西。
photographer/fəˈtɒɡrəfə(r)/n. 摄影师
His father is a good photographer and there are many beautiful photos in his home.
他父亲是一位优秀的摄影师，家里有许多美丽的照片。
architect/ˈɑːkɪtekt/n. 建筑师
An architect designs buildings and bridges in the city.
建筑师设计城市里的建筑和桥梁。
lawyer/ˈlɔːjə(r)/n. 律师
Su Yan wants to be a lawyer, so she is going to study law at university.
苏燕想当律师，所以她打算在大学学法律。
creative/kriˈeɪtɪv/adj. 有创造力的
Our art teacher is very creative and has many new ideas.
我们的美术老师很有创造力，有很多新点子。
energetic/ˌenəˈdʒetɪk/adj. 精力充沛的
He is an energetic boy and always plays sports after school.
他是个精力充沛的男孩，放学后总是运动。
confident/ˈkɒnfɪdənt/adj. 自信的

搭配 be confident in 对……有信心。

She is confident in winning the competition because she practices a lot.
她对赢得比赛很有信心，因为她练习很多。
patient/ˈpeɪʃnt/adj. 有耐心的
She is a patient teacher and never gets angry with her students.
她是一位有耐心的老师，从不和学生们生气。
purpose/ˈpɜːpəs/n. 目的；意图
What is the purpose of your visit to Beijing?
你去北京的目的是什么？
talent/ˈtælənt/n. 天赋；才能

搭配 have a talent for 在……有天赋。

Annie can dance well. It seems that she was born with a great talent for dancing.
安妮舞跳得很好。似乎她天生就有跳舞的天赋。
hero/ˈhɪərəʊ/n. 英雄；偶像
Zhong Nanshan is a great hero in many people's eyes.
钟南山在许多人眼中是一位伟大的英雄。
dream/driːm/n. 梦想 · v. 做梦
If you want to achieve your dream, you need to work hard and never give up.
如果你想实现梦想，你需要努力工作，永不放弃。

中考常考考点与易错点

1–10
1
考点一：be going to 结构

be going to + 动词原形；be 随主语变化；否定在 be 后加 not。

Jack, with his friends, is going to watch a new film at the cinema tomorrow.
杰克和他的朋友们明天打算去电影院看一部新电影。
2
考点二：there be 将来时

there is/are going to be 或 there will be，不能与 have 连用。

There are going to be two concerts tomorrow evening.
明天晚上将有两场音乐会。
3
考点三：现在进行时表将来

表示按计划即将发生的动作，常与将来时间状语连用。

The school art festival is coming. Let's design a logo for it.
学校艺术节就要到了。我们为它设计一个标志吧。
4
考点四：will 与 be going to 区别

be going to 强调计划/安排；will 强调单纯将来或临时决定。

—Are you going to be free tomorrow? —No. I will be free the day after tomorrow.
——你明天有空吗？——没有。我后天有空。
5
考点五：职业词汇辨析

pilot、engineer、designer、violinist、pianist、scientist、photographer、architect、lawyer。注意冠词 a/an。

—What are you going to be when you grow up? —An engineer. I'm going to study math hard.
——你长大后打算做什么？——一名工程师。我打算努力学数学。
—What does your aunt do? —She is a pilot, she often flies the plane to different countries.
——你姑姑是做什么的？——她是一名飞行员，她经常开飞机去不同的国家。
6
考点六：动词短语辨析

take up 开始从事；give up 放弃；put out 扑灭；put off 推迟；look up 查阅；clean up 打扫。

I'm going to take up painting as my new hobby next year.
我打算明年开始把绘画作为我的新爱好。
To put out the big fire in the forest, the firefighters worked hard for about four hours.
为了扑灭森林大火，消防员们努力工作了大约四个小时。
7
考点七：名词与形容词辨析

resolution、relationship、purpose、talent（名词）；creative、energetic、confident、patient（形容词）。

She made a resolution to read more books to improve her reading skills.
她下定决心多读书来提高阅读能力。
8
考点八：so that 目的状语从句

so that 以便，从句常含 can/could/may/might。

He studies hard so that he can get into a famous university.
他努力学习，以便能考上一所名牌大学。
9
考点九：be sure to do 与 be confident in

be sure to do 一定会做；be confident in 对……有信心。

You should be confident in yourself and never lose hope.
你应该对自己有信心，永远不要失去希望。
10
考点十：情景交际

问梦想 What's your dream job?；问计划 What are you going to do?；同意 I agree；安慰 Don't worry；回答道歉 It doesn't matter。

—What are you going to be when you grow up? —I'm not sure. Maybe a teacher is a good choice.
——你长大后打算做什么？——我不确定。也许当老师是个不错的选择。

语法易错点总结

姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. be going to 后接动词原形。
  2. be 动词随主语变化：I am / he is / they are。
  3. there be 将来时用 there is/are going to be，不与 have 连用。
  4. 现在进行时可表按计划即将发生。
  5. be going to 强调计划，will 强调临时决定。
  6. 职业词汇注意冠词 a/an。
  7. take up / give up / put out / put off / look up 区分含义。
  8. 名词与形容词区分词性。
  9. so that 从句常含情态动词。
  10. be sure to do 与 be confident in 区分。
  11. 情景交际：What's your dream job? / How are you going to do that?
  12. information 不可数，resolution 可数。
  13. want to do / dream is to do 不定式。
  14. make a resolution to do sth. to 后接动词原形。
  15. take up 后接名词或动名词。
