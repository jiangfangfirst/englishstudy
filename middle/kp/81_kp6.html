本 KP 三个关键：① be going to 后接动词原形；② there be 将来时用 there is/are going to be，不能用 have；③ be going to 强调计划，will 强调临时决定。

语法核心

本单元两大语法
语法一：be going to 结构

be going to do sth. 表示"打算/计划做某事"，be 动词随主语变化（I am / he is / they are），to 后接动词原形。否定在 be 后加 not；疑问把 be 提到主语前。

there be 将来时：there is/are going to be 或 there will be，不能与 have 连用，be 与最近主语一致。现在进行时表将来：表示按计划即将发生的动作。

I'm going to visit my grandparents this weekend.
There is going to be a talent show in ten minutes.
I am leaving for Beijing tomorrow.
语法二：will 表示将来

will + 动词原形，常与 tomorrow、next week、in the future 连用。be going to 强调计划/已有安排；will 强调单纯将来或临时决定。

I will make a delicious cake for my mother.
I'm going to take up a new hobby next term.（计划）
I will help you with your homework.（临时决定）

中考常考考点与易错点

1–10
1
考点一：be going to 结构

be going to + 动词原形；be 随主语变化；否定在 be 后加 not。

I'm going to visit my grandparents this weekend.
2
考点二：there be 将来时

there is/are going to be 或 there will be，不能与 have 连用。

There are going to be two football matches tomorrow.
3
考点三：现在进行时表将来

表示按计划即将发生的动作，常与将来时间状语连用。

I am leaving for Beijing tomorrow.
4
考点四：will 与 be going to 区别

be going to 强调计划/安排；will 强调单纯将来或临时决定。

I'm going to take up a new hobby next term.
5
考点五：职业词汇辨析

pilot、engineer、designer、violinist、pianist、scientist、photographer、architect、lawyer。注意冠词 a/an。

I want to be an engineer. / He is a pilot.
6
考点六：动词短语辨析

take up 开始从事；give up 放弃；put out 扑灭；put off 推迟；look up 查阅；clean up 打扫。

He is going to take up a new hobby.
The firemen tried their best to put out the fire.
7
考点七：名词与形容词辨析

resolution、relationship、purpose、talent（名词）；creative、energetic、confident、patient（形容词）。

My New Year's resolution is to improve my English.
8
考点八：so that 目的状语从句

so that 以便，从句常含 can/could/may/might。

He gets up early so that he can catch the early bus.
9
考点九：be sure to do 与 be confident in

be sure to do 一定会做；be confident in 对……有信心。

Tony works so hard and he is sure to succeed.
10
考点十：情景交际

问梦想 What's your dream job?；问计划 What are you going to do?；同意 I agree；安慰 Don't worry；回答道歉 It doesn't matter。

— What are you going to be when you grow up? — I'm going to be a doctor.

姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. be going to 后接动词原形。
  2. be 动词随主语变化：I am / he is / they are。
  3. there be 将来时用 there is/are going to be，不与 have 连用。
  4. 现在进行时可表按计划即将发生。
  5. be going to 强调计划，will 强调临时决定。
  6. 职业词汇注意冠词 a/an。
  7. take up / give up / put out / put off / look up 区分含义。
  8. 名词与形容词区分词性。
  9. so that 从句常含情态动词。
  10. be sure to do 与 be confident in 区分。
  11. 情景交际：What's your dream job? / How are you going to do that?
  12. information 不可数，resolution 可数。
  13. want to do / dream is to do 不定式。
  14. make a resolution to do sth. to 后接动词原形。
  15. take up 后接名词或动名词。
