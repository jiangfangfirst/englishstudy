语法核心

本单元两大语法
被动语态（三大时态）

一般现在时被动：am/is/are + done（is given、are sent）；一般过去时被动：was/were + done（was launched、was built）；一般将来时被动：will be + done（will be built、will be launched、will be sent、will be found、will be solved）。

被动语态强调动作承受者，动作执行者用 by 引出。

The spaceship will be designed by the engineers next year.
The International Space Station was built by several countries together.
现在完成时（so far / since then）

现在完成时：have/has + 过去分词；常与 so far（到目前为止）、since then（自那以后）、in the past fifty years（在过去的五十年里）连用。

So far, humans have walked on the moon several times.
Many new technologies have been developed through space exploration in the past fifty years.
情态动词被动与 whether/if 宾语从句

情态动词被动：must be dealt with、can be seen、may be found。

whether/if 引导宾语从句表“是否”，与 or not 连用只用 whether。

Space pollution must be dealt with well in the future.
We don't know whether there is life on other planets.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
一般现在时被动

is/are + 过去分词；主语单复数决定 is/are。

The space station can be seen in the sky at night if the weather is clear.
2
一般过去时被动

was/were + 过去分词；常与过去时间连用。

This spacecraft was launched in 1977.
3
一般将来时被动

will be + 过去分词；不用 will is/was。

Better ways to travel in space will be invented.
4
情态动词被动

must/can/may/should be + 过去分词。

The problem will be dealt with new methods.
5
现在完成时 so far / since then

have/has + 过去分词；so far 常与现在完成时连用。

So far, humans have walked on the moon several times.
6
in the past fifty years

in the past + 时间段 与现在完成时连用。

Many new technologies have been developed through space exploration in the past fifty years.
7
whether/if 宾语从句

表“是否”；与 or not 连用只能用 whether。

The space trip will be cancelled because of bad weather tomorrow.
8
lift off / set foot on / carry out

lift off（火箭）升空；set foot on 踏上；carry out 执行任务。

The rocket lifted off into the sky, carrying the satellite.
9
be proud of / be equipped with

be proud of 为……自豪；be equipped with 装备有（with 介词）。

The whole nation is proud of the astronauts who made the mission a success.
10
词性转换：explore/exploration

explore v. 探索；exploration n.；explorer n. 探险家。

It takes great courage to become a successful astronaut.
11
情景交际——表达期待

I can't wait to do sth. 等不及做某事；Sounds great 表赞同。

—I'm going to the space museum tomorrow. —Have a good time!
12
情景交际——表可能

Maybe/Perhaps 表可能；Of course 表当然。

—Will the sports meet be held next week? —Yes, it will.

语法易错点总结

考前必看
!
姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. 被动语态将来时：will be + 过去分词，will 后不接 is/are。
  2. have/has + 过去分词，过去分词不随主语变化。
  3. so far / since then / in the past fifty years 均与现在完成时连用。
  4. whether...or not 固定搭配，不能用 if...or not。
  5. spacecraft 单复数同形，spacecrafts 是错误形式。
  6. set foot on 踏上，foot 前不加冠词（set foot on the moon）。
  7. lift off 主语是火箭/飞船，不是人。
  8. be equipped with 用 with 引出装备，不接 by。
  9. bring about 带来（变化），take place 发生（不及物）。
  10. in order to 后接动词原形，表目的。
  11. fall asleep 入睡（短暂动作），be asleep 睡着（状态）。
  12. carry out 执行（任务/计划），代词放中间：carry it out。
