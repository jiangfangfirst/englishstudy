语法核心

by doing · how 疑问句
by + doing 结构

by 是介词，表"通过……方式、靠……"，后接名词、代词或动名词，作方式状语，回答 how 疑问句。

Many villagers make money by selling fruits online.
Many old people keep in touch with their relatives by using mobile phones now.
对 by doing 提问用 how

问学习方式用 how，答语用 by doing。

—How do you improve your English? —By reading English newspapers every day.

The best way to do sth. is by doing sth. 表"做某事的最好方法"。

The best way to remember new words is by using word cards.
by 的其他用法

by 还可表交通、位置、时间，但此处重点考"方式"。by 后接动名词，不能接原形或不定式。

My father goes to work by bus every day.
how 引导的特殊疑问句

how 询问方式、程度、数量等，此处主要考询问"方式"。

—How long have you studied English? —Since six years ago.
—How do you like your new school? —It's much bigger than my old one.

how 与 many、much、long、often、soon、far 构成词组，分别询问数量、价格、时长、频率、将来时间、距离，据答语判断。

—How long have you lived in this city? —For about five years.
—How long have you known your grandpa? —For about 20 years. He became a good friend of my family.

重点词汇精讲

1–10
textbook/ˈtekstbʊk/n. 教科书；课本

由 text（文本）+ book（书）构成，指学校使用的课本。

Please turn to page ten in your English textbook.
aloud/əˈlaʊd/adv. 出声地；大声地

强调"发出声音地读"，常与 read 搭配指朗读。辨析：aloud 出声地（read aloud 大声朗读）· loud 形/副（多与 speak、talk 连用）· loudly 副词含喧闹意味（与 shout、knock 连用）。

I am used to getting up early in the morning to read English aloud, but I used to hate it when I was in primary school.
常考：read aloud（朗读）与 read loudly（大声读，带嘈杂感）不同，常在语境考查。
pronunciation/prəˌnʌnsiˈeɪʃn/n. 发音；读音

动词 pronounce；名词拼写去掉 o（pronounce → pronunciation）。

Reading aloud can help you improve your pronunciation.
常考：名词拼写是单词拼写高频考点，易误写为 "pronounciation"。
patient/ˈpeɪʃnt/adj. 有耐心的 · n. 病人

形容词搭配 be patient with sb./sth.；作名词指"病人"。派生词 patience n.、patiently adv.

Our English teacher is patient with every student.
常考：be patient with 固定搭配考介词填空；注意作名词表"病人"，据语境判断。
discover/dɪˈskʌvə(r)/v. 发现；发觉

发现已存在但未知的事物。辨析：discover 发现（原本存在未知）· invent 发明（原本不存在）· find out 查明（调查研究）· create 创造（从无到有）。

—Good news! A team of scientists have discovered a new type of ocean creature in the deep sea. —Wow! It will help us learn more about the ocean.
常考：四词辨析是中考高频选择题考点。
knowledge/ˈnɒlɪdʒ/n. 知识；学问

不可数名词，无复数形式。搭配 knowledge of sth.（关于……的知识）· a good knowledge of（对……很了解）。

The world is changing fast, and we should keep learning new knowledge.
陷阱：不能说 a knowledge 或 knowledges，但可说 a good knowledge of；单选常设 a knowledge 干扰项。
wisely/ˈwaɪzli/adv. 明智地；聪明地

形容词 wise，名词 wisdom。

We should use our pocket money wisely.
常考：wise → wisely 副词变化，及 wisdom 拼写。
ability/əˈbɪləti/n. 能力；才能

搭配 the ability to do sth. · have the ability to do sth.（有能力做某事）。

She has the ability to learn foreign languages quickly.
常考：the ability to do sth. 后接不定式，不接动名词。
active/ˈæktɪv/adj. 活跃的；积极的

搭配 be active in（在……方面积极）· take an active part in（积极参加）。派生词 activity n.、actively adv.

All the students are active in the English corner.
常考：take an active part in 是短语填空高频考点。
connect/kəˈnekt/v. （使）连接；与……有联系

搭配 connect A with B（把 A 和 B 联系起来）· connect A to B（把 A 连接到 B）。派生词 connection n.

The bridge connects the village with the town.
常考：connect...with... 与 connect...to... 都是固定搭配，常考介词选择。

中考常考考点与易错点

1–20
1
考点一：by doing 的考查

常以单选或句型转换考查。陷阱：by 后接动名词，不能接原形或不定式；对 by doing 提问用 how。

—How do you improve your English? —By reading English newspapers every day.
2
考点二：patient 的用法

be patient with 固定搭配考介词；patient 作名词表"病人"，据语境判断；patience 是名词"耐心"。

You should be patient when you learn a new language.
The kind nurse looked after the old patient carefully.
3
考点三：discover / invent / find out / create 辨析

四词都与"发现、创造"有关，意义不同，中考常设语境选择。

—Good news! A team of scientists have discovered a new type of ocean creature in the deep sea. —Wow! It will help us learn more about the ocean.
Edison invented the light bulb over a hundred years ago.
Please find out when the last train leaves tonight.
The artist created many beautiful paintings last year.
4
考点四：mistake 的用法

可数名词。make mistakes（复数泛指）、make a mistake（具体一个）、make mistakes in、by mistake（错误地）。

Don't be afraid of making mistakes in class.
5
考点五：attention 的用法

pay attention to 中 to 是介词，后接名词、代词或动名词；易误接动词原形。

Please pay attention to reading the questions first.
6
考点六：ability 的用法

the ability to do sth. 后接不定式，不接动名词。

He has the ability to finish the difficult task alone.
7
考点七：knowledge 的用法

不可数名词，不能加 a 或 -s，但可说 a good knowledge of。

In my opinion, online learning makes it easier for students to get knowledge anytime.
8
考点八：后接动名词的动词

finish / practice / enjoy / mind / keep 等后只能接动名词，不接不定式。

The world is changing fast, and we should keep learning new knowledge.
9
考点九：instead of 的用法

instead of 是介词短语，后接名词、代词或动名词；instead 是副词，单独使用。

I walked to school instead of taking the bus today.
10
考点十：so that 与 so...that... 的区别

so that 表目的（从句常含 can/could/may/might）；so...that... 表结果（so 接形/副）。

Many young people return to their villages so that they can develop farming business.
—The path(小路) in the mountains is so rough that we need to walk carefully to avoid falling. —You're right! It's full of stones and holes.
11
考点十一：even if 与 even though

都表"即使"，但 even if 侧重假设，even though 侧重事实，常考状语从句连词选择。

Trees can help keep soil in place even if there is a storm.
Even though it rained heavily, we still went out.
12
考点十二：such...that... 与 so...that...

such 后接名词短语，so 后接形容词或副词。

It is such a useful book that I read it twice.
—The path(小路) in the mountains is so rough that we need to walk carefully to avoid falling. —You're right! It's full of stones and holes.
13
考点十三：find 与 look for 的辨析

find 强调"找到"的结果；look for 强调"寻找"的动作。

I looked for my pen everywhere, but I couldn't find it.
Many young people leave their hometowns to look for better jobs in big cities.
14
考点十四：be afraid to do 与 be afraid of doing

都表"害怕做某事"，但前者强调不敢做，后者强调担心后果。

The shy boy is afraid to speak in front of the class.
She is afraid of making mistakes when she speaks English.
15
考点十五：be active in 与 take an active part in

be active in 表"在……方面积极"；take an active part in 表"积极参加"。

We are active in all kinds of after-school activities.
Many students take an active part in the sports meeting.
16
考点十六：connect...to... 与 connect...with...

connect...to... 表"把……连接到……"；connect...with... 表"把……和……联系起来"。

Please connect the printer to the computer first.
We often connect spring with new life and hope.
17
考点十七：be worth doing

表"值得做"，后接动名词，不接不定式。

The movie is worth watching with your parents.
18
考点十八：be good at 与 be good with

be good at 表"擅长"；be good with 表"与……相处好"。

Lucy is good at maths. She can solve the difficult maths problem with ease.
Mr. Li is good with children, and they all like him.
19
考点十九：successful / succeed / success

success 名词、succeed 动词、successful 形容词、successfully 副词。

He succeeded in developing a new kind of high-tech product after years of hard work.

come 后接形容词 successful 作表语；will 后接动词原形 succeed。

20
考点二十：the secret to

the secret to... 表"……的秘诀"，to 是介词，后接名词或动名词。

The secret to learning English is to use it every day.

重点句型

经典句型
1

2
—How do you improve your English? —By reading English newspapers every day.
3

4

5

it 作形式宾语，真正宾语是不定式。
6

7

语法易错点总结

考前必看
!
姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. by doing 中的 doing 不能换成原形或不定式。
  2. 对 by doing 提问用 how，不用 what。
  3. patient 作形容词搭配 with，作名词表"病人"；patience 是名词"耐心"。
  4. discover、invent、find out、create 结合语境辨析。
  5. pay attention to 中 to 是介词，后接动名词。
  6. the ability to do sth. 接不定式，不接动名词。
  7. finish、practice、enjoy、mind、keep 等后接动名词。
  8. instead of 后接动名词，instead 单独使用。
  9. so that 表示目的，so...that... 表示结果。
  10. knowledge 不可数，不能加 a 或 -s，但可说 a good knowledge of。
  11. mistake 可数，make mistakes 复数泛指，make a mistake 指具体一个。
  12. 句型转换中 by doing 与 how 提问是常考配对。
  13. be afraid to do 和 be afraid of doing 都表害怕，但侧重不同。
  14. be active in 和 take an active part in 都是常考短语。
  15. connect...to... 和 connect...with... 都是固定搭配。
  16. be worth doing 后接动名词。
  17. be good at 表擅长，be good with 表与……相处好。
  18. success 是名词，succeed 是动词，successful 是形容词。
  19. the secret to 中 to 是介词，后接名词或动名词。
  20. such...that... 中 such 接名词短语，so...that... 中 so 接形容词或副词。
