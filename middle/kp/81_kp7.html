八年级上册 · KP 7

要点：① will + 动词原形表将来（否定 won't）；② be going to 表计划/有迹象；③ there will be / there is going to be（不与 have 连用）；④ if 条件句主将从现；⑤ 现在进行时表按计划即将发生。
will 将来时will do · won't · Will...?
be going to计划 · 有迹象 · be+going to do
未来预测robot · pollution · future · replace
本 KP 三个关键：① will 与 be going to 后都接动词原形；② there be 将来时不与 have 连用；③ if 条件句主句将来、从句现在（主将从现）。

语法核心

本单元四大语法
语法一：一般将来时 will

will + 动词原形，常与 tomorrow、next week、in the future、in 100 years 连用。否定 will not = won't；疑问把 will 提到主语前。

Wait a moment, please. The party will start after everyone arrives.
—Do you think people will live on Mars in 50 years? —Maybe. Scientists are working hard on space travel.
—Will there be less pollution in the future? —I hope so. We should protect the environment now.
语法二：be going to 结构

be going to + 动词原形，表打算/计划，或根据迹象即将发生。be 随主语变化。be going to 强调计划/安排；will 强调单纯将来或临时决定。

—How will you spend your summer vacation next year? —I'm going to visit the space museum in Beijing.
It may rain this evening. The sky is getting dark and cloudy.
语法三：there be 句型的将来时

用 there will be 或 there is/are going to be，不能与 have 连用。be 与最近主语一致。

There will be a meeting next Monday afternoon.
There is going to be a basketball game between Class 1 and Class 3 tomorrow.
语法四：现在进行时表示将来

现在进行时可表示按计划即将发生的动作，常与将来时间状语连用。

We are glad to hear that the Greens will move to a new flat next week.
—Chris, what will you do next Sunday? —I will visit the Science Museum with my friends.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–6
1
考点一：will 与 be going to 区别

be going to 强调计划/安排；will 强调单纯将来或临时决定。

I've made my decision. I am going to study abroad next year.
—I'm afraid I can't finish the work alone. —Don't worry. I will help you.
2
考点二：there be 将来时

there will be 或 there is/are going to be，不与 have 连用。

There will be a big sports meeting in our city next month.
3
考点三：if 条件状语从句主将从现

if 引导条件状语从句，主句用一般将来时，从句用一般现在时表将来。

If you study hard, you will get good grades.
4
考点四：现在进行时表将来

按计划即将发生的动作，常与将来时间状语连用。

We are glad to hear that the Greens will move to a new flat next week.
5
考点五：词汇辨析

pollution、prediction、future、space、robot、replace、research（不可数）、cure、accept、mention、quality、positive、challenging、depend。

There will be fewer jobs for people because some robots will do the same jobs as people.
6
考点六：情景交际

问未来 What will the future be like?；同意 I agree；希望 I hope so；安慰 Don't worry。

—What will the world be like in 100 years? —Maybe there will be more robots everywhere.

语法易错点总结

姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. will 后接动词原形。
  2. be going to 后接动词原形。
  3. there be 将来时不与 have 连用。
  4. if 条件句：主句将来，从句现在（主将从现）。
  5. 现在进行时可表按计划即将发生。
  6. be going to 强调计划，will 强调临时决定。
  7. research 不可数，prediction 可数。
  8. depend on / come true / learn about / play a part in 区分。
  9. positive / challenging 等形容词用法。
  10. 情景交际：I hope so. / I agree. / Don't worry.
  11. there be 句型 be 动词就近原则。
  12. will / be going to 的疑问结构。
  13. as long as 引导条件状语从句"只要"。
