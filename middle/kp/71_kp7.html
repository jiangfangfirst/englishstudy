语法核心

本单元两大语法
序数词

构成：first / second / third 特殊；fourth、fifth（ve→f+th）、twelfth（lve→lf+th）；整十变 y 为 ie：twentieth；其余加 -th：sixth、seventh、eighth、ninth、tenth。

序数词前通常加 the：the first day。

January is the first month of the year.
—Tree Planting Day is on the twelfth of March. —It's the best time to plant trees.
日期表达

询问日期：What's the date today?（几月几号）vs What day is it today?（星期几）。

具体日期前用 on：on May 1st；月份前用 in：in May。

What is the date today? It's 10 October.
My birthday is on July 3rd.
How old / How much / How many

How old 问年龄 · How much 问价格（不可数数量）· How many 问数量（可数名词复数，后接复数名词 + are there）。

—How old is your sister? —She's eight.
—How much is this birthday cake? —It's 100 yuan.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
考点一：序数词特殊拼写

first / second / third 特殊；fifth（ve→f+th）、twelfth（lve→lf+th）、twentieth（ty→tie+th）。

Tony is twelve years old and this is his twelfth birthday.
2
考点二：序数词前加 the

序数词前通常加 the；日期表达 May 1st 可省 the。

The first month of the year is January.
3
考点三：日期问答

What's the date today? 问几月几号；回答 It's + 月份 + 序数词。

What is the date today? It's 10 October.
4
考点四：星期问答

What day is it today? 问星期几；回答 It's Monday.

I have an orange sweater for my birthday.
5
考点五：on / in 表日期

具体日期前用 on：on May 1st；月份/年用 in：in May, in 2026。

My birthday is on 8th August.
6
考点六：How old 问年龄

How old are you? 回答 I'm + 数字（+ years old）。

—How old is your sister? —She's eight.
7
考点七：How much 问价格

How much is...? 询问价格；可数复数 How much are...?。

—This birthday cake is so beautiful. How much is it? —It's 85 yuan.
8
考点八：How many 问数量

How many + 可数名词复数 + are there...? 询问数量。

We put twelve candles on the birthday cake.
9
考点九：buy sth. for sb.

buy sth. for sb. = buy sb. sth. 为某人买某物。

I want to buy a gift for my friend's birthday.
10
考点十：make a wish 与 blow out

make a wish 许愿；blow out the candles 吹灭蜡烛。

Before eating the cake, let's blow out the candles on it.
11
考点十一：购物情景交际

Can I help you? 店员招呼；How much...? 问价；What's the total? 问总价。

—Can I help you? —Yes, please. How much is the cake?
12
考点十二：Here is... for you.

Here is a present for you. 这是给你的礼物（递物用语）。

My friends sing the "Happy Birthday" song for me.

语法易错点总结

姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. 序数词特殊拼写：first、second、third、fifth、twelfth、twentieth。
  2. 序数词前通常加 the：the first day。
  3. 问日期用 What's the date?，问星期用 What day is it?。
  4. 具体日期前用 on：on May 1st；月份前用 in：in May。
  5. How old 问年龄，How much 问价格，How many 问数量（接复数）。
  6. buy sth. for sb. 为某人买某物。
  7. make a wish 许愿；blow out 吹灭。
  8. Can I help you? 是店员招呼语，顾客回答 I'd like...。
  9. What's the total? 问总价。
  10. Here is... for you. 递物用语。
  11. December 是十二月，第十二是 twelfth。
  12. Happy birthday to you! 生日快乐。
