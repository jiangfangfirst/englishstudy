八年级上册 · KP 1

要点：① 复合不定代词 some-/any-/no-/every- 的用法；② 一般过去时构成与 was/were；③ 形容词修饰复合不定代词后置；④ 假期活动话题情景交际。
复合不定代词something · anything · nothing
一般过去时did / was · were · -ed
假期话题vacation · camp · fantastic
本 KP 三个关键：① 肯定句用 some-、否定疑问用 any-；② 形容词修饰不定代词必须后置；③ 一般过去时否定疑问用 didn't / Did + 动词原形。

语法核心

本单元两大语法
语法一：复合不定代词

由 some-/any-/no-/every- 加 -body/-one/-thing 构成，作主语时谓语用单数。some- 类多用于肯定句；any- 类多用于否定句、疑问句和条件从句；表示请求、建议或希望得到肯定回答的疑问句仍用 some-。形容词、else 或不定式修饰时必须后置。

—Did you buy anything special? —Yes, I bought some souvenirs.
Would you like something to drink, Lily? I think you are thirsty.
There is something wrong with my watch. It doesn't work.
—Who taught you to fix a bike? It looks so professional! —Nobody. I learned it from videos.
语法二：一般过去时

表示过去某个时间发生的动作或状态，常与 yesterday、last summer、two days ago、in 2010 等连用。规则动词加 -ed；不规则动词需单独记忆。否定句用 didn't + 动词原形，疑问句用 Did + 主语 + 动词原形。be 动词：I/he/she/it/单数用 was；you/we/they/复数用 were。

—Where did you go on vacation? —I went to the mountains.
—Where did you go for your holiday? —I went to the mountains with my friends.
—Did you try any special food on vacation? —Yes, I did.

重点词汇精讲

1–20
vacation/vəˈkeɪʃn/n. 假期；度假

美式英语，英式常用 holiday。搭配 go on vacation 去度假。

—Where did you go on vacation last winter? —I flew to Hainan with my family and had a good time there.

搭配 go to summer camp 去夏令营。

Everyone in our class went to the summer camp, so the classroom was empty.

Luckily, the little boy found his way back to the hotel with the help of the guide.

scarf/skɑːf/n. 围巾

复数形式为 scarves。

comfortable/ˈkʌmftəbl/adj. 舒适的

副词形式 comfortably。

—How do you feel about the new chair? —It's really comfortable. I can sit in it for a long time without feeling tired.

修饰人，常与 with 连用；boring 修饰物。

ready/ˈredi/adj. 准备好的；愿意的

搭配 be ready to do sth. 愿意做某事；be ready for sth. 为……做好准备。

We aren't ready for the match, while they get everything ready to play it.
somewhere/ˈsʌmweə(r)/adv. 在某处；到某处
—Next month we're going somewhere interesting as soon as the holiday begins. —Sounds good!
anywhere/ˈeniweə(r)/adv. 在任何地方
Did you go anywhere interesting during your summer holiday?
nothing/ˈnʌθɪŋ/pron. 没有任何东西
—What did you do on your holiday? —I did nothing special. I just read books at home.
fantastic/fænˈtæstɪk/adj. 极好的；吸引人的
Chinese tea is fantastic. Many foreigners love it a lot.
breath/breθ/n. 呼吸的空气；一口气

搭配 take one's breath away 令人惊叹。

—How was the scenery of Mount Yulong? —It just took my breath away.
remind/rɪˈmaɪnd/v. 提醒；使想起

搭配 remind sb. of sth. 使某人想起；remind sb. to do sth. 提醒某人做某事。

This old photo reminds me of my happy childhood.
fight/faɪt/v./n. 战斗；打架

过去式 fought。搭配 fight against 与……作斗争。

The sisters often have a fight over who gets to use the computer first.
peace/piːs/n. 和平；太平
People all over the world love peace and hope for a happy life.
probably/ˈprɒbəbli/adv. 很可能；大概
It will probably rain tomorrow, so take an umbrella with you.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–14
1
考点一：复合不定代词的选用

肯定句用 some- 类，否定句和疑问句用 any- 类；表示请求、建议或希望得到肯定回答的疑问句用 some- 类。

—Did you do anything interesting on your holiday? —No, I just stayed at home and helped with some housework.
—Would you like something to eat? —Yes, some bread, please.
2
考点二：形容词修饰复合不定代词后置

形容词、else、不定式修饰复合不定代词时必须后置。

—Did you buy anything special when you were in Hong Kong, Dad? —Yes, I bought a toy robot for you.
I have something important to tell you.
3
考点三：复合不定代词作主语谓语用单数

复合不定代词作主语时，谓语动词用单数。

—How is it going, Sally? —Everything is excellent.
Nobody knows everything about him, because he never tells anything about his private life.
4
考点四：no- 类与 every- 类

no- 类表示否定含义；every- 类表示"全部、人人"。

—Mrs. Williams, what's your secret of being a wonderful tennis player? —Nothing special. I just work hard every day.
Everyone in our class had a good time on the school trip.
5
考点五：一般过去时的否定句和疑问句

否定句用 didn't + 动词原形；疑问句用 Did + 主语 + 动词原形。

He didn't watch TV last night. He read books.
—Did you eat anything special in that restaurant? —Yes. I had some delicious Beijing duck.
6
考点六：was 与 were 的选用

主语是 I/he/she/it 或单数名词用 was；主语是 you/we/they 或复数名词用 were。

It was rainy last weekend, so we stayed at home.
There were a lot of people on the beach yesterday afternoon.
7
考点七：反身代词的用法

反身代词与主语一致，常考 teach oneself、enjoy oneself、by oneself、keep oneself safe。

—Tom, you played the piano very well at the concert. Who taught you? —Nobody. I taught myself.
—Did you enjoy yourselves at the party? —Yes, we did.
8
考点八：how 询问假期情况

How was your holiday? 回答用 It was fantastic / great / wonderful / terrible.

—How was your vacation? —Pretty good! I climbed the Great Wall with my friends.
9
考点九：询问假期去向与活动

Where did you go on vacation? 问去向；What did you do during the holiday? 问活动。

—Where did you go on vacation? —I went to a small town near Shanghai.
10
考点十：take one's breath away

表示"令人惊叹；让人叹绝"。

—How was the scenery of Mount Yulong? —It just took my breath away.
11
考点十一：remind 的用法

remind sb. of sth. 使某人想起；remind sb. to do sth. 提醒某人做某事。

Please remind me to close the window when I leave.
12
考点十二：nothing but 与 ready to do

nothing but 只有；只是；ready to do sth. 愿意做某事。

We aren't ready for the match, while they get everything ready to play it.
13
考点十三：be surprised to do sth.

表示"很惊讶做某事"。

—Tom bought me a big birthday cake. It was a big surprise for me. —Yeah, we were all surprised at it at the party.
14
考点十四：情景交际

询问感受 How was your holiday?；询问去向 Where did you go on vacation?；询问活动 What did you do...?。

—Peter, did you play table tennis with your friends after school yesterday? —No, I didn't. We watered vegetables in our school garden.
