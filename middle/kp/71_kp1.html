语法核心

本单元两大语法
be 动词 am / is / are

be 动词随主语变化：I am、you / we / they 用 are、he / she / it 及单数名词用 is。否定句在 be 后加 not，疑问句把 be 提到主语前。

be 动词还用于表达年龄、国籍、职业、所在位置等：I am twelve. / She is from China.

Tony is in Class 2, Grade 7.
—Are you a student? —Yes, I am.
—Are you in Class Two? —No, I'm not. I'm in Class Three.
人称代词主格与形容词性物主代词

主格作主语：I / you / he / she / it / we / you / they；形容词性物主代词后必须接名词：my / your / his / her / its / our / your / their。

易混配对：I→my、you→your、he→his、she→her、it→its、we→our、they→their。

This is my friend. His name is Alan.
Mike's aunt is our English teacher. We all like her.
姓名与国籍表达

英文姓名名在前姓在后：first name 名、last name / family name 姓。询问姓名：What's your name?；询问来自哪里：Where are you from? 或 Where do you come from?

国籍表达：be + 形容词（Chinese / American / English...），如 She is Chinese.

—What's your name? —My name is Amy.
—Where is he from? —He is from the United States.

重点词汇精讲

1–10
name/neɪm/n. 名字；姓名

What's your name? 询问姓名；full name 全名。

My name is Tom.
常考问句用 What's your name?，回答用 My name is... 或 I'm...。
first/fɜːst/adj. 第一的；n. 第一个

first name 名（名字），位于姓之前。

My new classmate's name is Mike Miller. Mike is his first name.
易错first name 是“名”，last name / family name 才是“姓”。
last/lɑːst/adj. 最后的；上一个

last name / family name 姓。

The girl is Helen Green. Green is her last name.
classmate/ˈklɑːsmeɪt/n. 同班同学

class（班级）+ mate（同伴）= classmate。

—Who is he? —He is my new classmate.
country/ˈkʌntri/n. 国家；乡村

be from + 国家 表来自某国。

Canada is a beautiful country, I want to go there for a vacation.
grade/ɡreɪd/n. 年级；等级

Grade 7 七年级；in Class 2, Grade 7 在七年级二班。

My grade is Grade 7, Class 2. We have a kind class teacher.
常考班级在前年级在后：Class 2, Grade 7。
class/klɑːs/n. 班级；课

in Class 2 在二班；have classes 上课。

We're in the same class.
friend/frend/n. 朋友

make friends with sb. 与某人交朋友。

Bob and Mike are good friends.
hobby/ˈhɒbi/n. 业余爱好

复数 hobbies。

—What's your hobby? —I like singing.
everyone/ˈevriwʌn/pron. 每人；人人

作主语时谓语用单数。

She is a new student. Let's say hello to her.
易错everyone 作主语谓语用单数，如 Everyone likes music.。

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
考点一：be 动词与主语搭配

I 用 am；you / we / they 用 are；he / she / it 及单数名词用 is。否定 be+not，疑问 be 提前。

I am a student. My name is Li Ming.
Those are my good friends.
2
考点二：一般疑问句的回答

Are you...? 肯定 Yes, I am.，否定 No, I'm not.（不能简写 I'mn't）；Is he...? 回答 Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.

—Are you a student? —Yes, I am.
—Are you Emma? —No, I'm not. I'm Ella.
3
考点三：形容词性物主代词后接名词

my / your / his / her / its / our / their 后必须接名词，不能单独使用。

—Is this your computer? —No, it's Lily's. Mine is on the table.
4
考点四：询问姓名

What's your name? 回答 My name is... 或 I'm...；问第三人称 What's his / her name?

—What's his name? —His name is Jack.
5
考点五：询问国籍与来自哪里

Where are you from? = Where do you come from? 回答 I'm from + 地点。

—Where is Mr Smith from? —He's from the US.
6
考点六：be from 与 come from

be from 后接地点名词；come from 中 come 为实义动词，疑问否定需借助助动词。

Betty is from the USA.
—Where are you from? —I'm from China.
7
考点七：年龄表达

How old...? 询问年龄；be + 数字 + years old。

Emma is 13 years old.
8
考点八：电话号码的表达

电话号码逐位读；询问 What's your telephone number?

—What's your phone number? —It's 139-0876-2345.
9
考点九：班级年级与地点表达

in Class 2, Grade 7（班级在前年级在后）；in 后接班级/年级/城市。

Tony is in Class 2, Grade 7.
Wang Yaming lives in Beijing with his big family.
10
考点十：everyone / everybody 作主语

复合不定代词作主语，谓语用单数。

One of my classmates is from America. He can't speak Chinese very well.
11
考点十一：情景交际——初次见面

Nice to meet you. 应答 Nice to meet you, too.；How do you do? 应答 How do you do?

—Nice to meet you! —Nice to meet you, too.
12
考点十二：thank 与 thanks

Thank you. = Thanks.；对感谢的应答 You're welcome. 不客气。

—Thank you very much. —You're welcome.

语法易错点总结

考前必看
!
姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. I 永远搭配 am，不能写成 I is / I are。
  2. you 无论指“你”还是“你们”，be 动词都用 are。
  3. 形容词性物主代词后必须接名词，不能单独作表语。
  4. first name 是“名”，last name / family name 是“姓”。
  5. 班级在前年级在后：Class 2, Grade 7。
  6. everyone / everybody 作主语谓语用单数。
  7. Are you...? 的否定回答是 No, I'm not.，不能写 No, I amn't.。
  8. Where are you from? 与 Where do you come from? 意思相同，但助动词不同。
  9. 电话号码逐位读，如 6833 读 six eight double three。
  10. Nice to meet you. 的应答是 Nice to meet you, too.。
  11. 国籍形容词作表语：She is Chinese.（不能说 She is China.）
  12. be from 后接地点名词，come from 中 from 也是介词。
