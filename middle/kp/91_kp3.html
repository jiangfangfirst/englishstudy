语法核心

本单元两大语法
宾语从句（一）：引导词与语序

宾语从句三要素：① 引导词 that（陈述句，可省略）、if/whether（一般疑问句）、特殊疑问词（特殊疑问句）；② 陈述语序（主语 + 谓语）；③ 时态呼应（主句过去时，从句一般用相应的过去时；客观真理用一般现在时）。

主句为一般现在时，从句可按需用任何时态；主句为一般过去时，从句用过去时态，但客观真理不受影响。

I believe that practice makes perfect in learning English.
The teacher asked us whether we had finished our homework or not.
宾语从句（二）：否定前置与 if/whether

think/believe/expect/suppose 等动词后接否定宾语从句时，否定前移到主句：I don't think...。

介词后、与 or not 连用、句首时只用 whether 不用 if；一般情况 if/whether 可互换。

I don't think he can finish the work in such a short time.
We are talking about whether we should have a group discussion tomorrow.
suggest doing · have trouble doing · the+比较级

suggest doing sth. 建议做某事；suggest + that 从句（should）do；suggest 后不接 to do。

have trouble/difficulty (in) doing sth. 做某事有困难。

the + 比较级，the + 比较级：越……，越……。

The teacher suggested reading aloud every day to improve our pronunciation.
The more we practise, the better we learn.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
宾语从句引导词 that

that 引导陈述句宾语从句，可省略；从句用陈述语序。

Mr. Wang finds that he can train his memory by drawing mind maps.
2
if / whether 辨析

与 or not 连用、作介词宾语、句首引导从句时只能用 whether。

I don't know whether he can take part in the research project or not.
3
否定前置

think/believe/expect 的否定宾语从句否定前移：I don't think...。

I don't think that speaking aloud is useful for pronunciation.
4
宾语从句时态呼应

主句过去时，从句用相应过去时；客观真理用一般现在时。

She said that she would go to Beijing the next week.
The teacher told us that the earth goes around the sun.
5
suggest doing / suggest that (should) do

suggest 后接 doing；接从句用 should do（should 可省略）；不接 sb. to do。

Mr Li suggests watching English movies to improve our listening.
6
have trouble (in) doing sth.

trouble/difficulty 后接 in doing，in 可省略。

Many students have trouble understanding English grammar. They need more practice.
7
the + 比较级，the + 比较级

表示“越……，越……”，两个比较级前都要加 the。

The harder you work, the greater progress you will make.
8
no matter what / whatever 让步状语从句

no matter what = whatever（无论什么），引导让步状语从句；主句一般不用将来时。

no matter what happens, it's best to remember the good things.
9
take advantage of / look up / come across

take advantage of 利用；look up 查阅（代词放中间）；come across 偶然遇到。

As a student, you should take advantage of the school library to read more good books.
10
词性转换：method/strategy/resource

method of doing；learning strategy；resource 复数 resources。

—How is your English study, Sarah? —It has improved a lot. Your method really works.
11
情景交际——感谢与应答

Thank you. 应答：You're welcome. / My pleasure. / Not at all.

—Thanks for giving me so much advice about how to learn English. —My pleasure.
12
情景交际——祝愿

Have a good time! 祝玩得开心；Good luck! 祝好运。

—I will have an English test tomorrow. —Good luck to you!

语法易错点总结

考前必看
!
姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. 宾语从句必须用陈述语序，疑问句结构要还原：He asked where I lived. 而非 where did I live.。
  2. if/whether 与 or not 连用时只能用 whether。
  3. think/believe 的否定要前移：I don't think he is right. 不写 I think he isn't right.。
  4. suggest 后不接 sb. to do；接 doing 或 that (should) do。
  5. have trouble/difficulty (in) doing sth.，介词 in 可省略。
  6. the + 比较级，the + 比较级 中两个 the 都不能少。
  7. no matter what/where/when 后主句不用将来时，用一般现在时表将来。
  8. look up 后接代词作宾语时放中间：look it up。
  9. come across 表“偶然遇到”，run into 同义。
  10. feedback 不可数，many feedback 是错的。
  11. efficient（高效）与 effective（有效）含义不同，勿混用。
  12. practice 是名词/动词，practise 英式动词；practice makes perfect 是谚语。
