语法核心

本单元两大语法
of / for + sb. + to do 句型

It is/was + adj. + of sb. to do sth. 中 of 前的形容词说明人物的品质、性格（kind、brave、wise、clever、nice、polite 等），可改写为 sb. is + adj. + to do sth.。

It is/was + adj. + for sb. to do sth. 中 for 前的形容词说明事情的性质（important、easy、difficult、possible、necessary、interesting 等），不能改写为 sb. is + adj.。

判断口诀：形容词描述“人”用 of，描述“事”用 for。

It was very brave of the firefighter to rush into the burning building.
消防员冲进燃烧的大楼，真是非常勇敢。
It's difficult for me to make more friends.
对我来说，交更多的朋友很困难。
强调句与主语从句

强调句结构：It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分，可强调主语、宾语、状语；去掉 It is...that 后句子依然完整。

主语从句：It is said / believed / reported / known that...（据说/人们相信/据报道/众所周知……），it 作形式主语，真正主语是 that 从句。

It was in the small park that we first met each other ten years ago.
十年前，我们就是在这个小公园第一次见面的。
—It is said that he climbed Mount Qomolangma at the age of 70. —Really? He is so amazing!
——据说他70岁时登上了珠穆朗玛峰。——真的吗？他太了不起了！
定语从句 · used to · 现在完成时

定语从句：先行词指人用 who/that（作主语）、whom/that（作宾语，可省略）；指物用 which/that；表“某人的”用 whose。

used to do sth. 过去常常做某事（现在不做了）；be used to doing sth. 习惯做某事；be used to do sth. 被用来做某事。

现在完成时：since + 过去时间点 / for + 时间段；yet 用于否定与疑问，already 用于肯定。

—Who is the person you look up to most? —My father. He is full of courage and wisdom.
——你最敬仰的人是谁？——我父亲。他充满勇气和智慧。
We didn't use to admire scientists, but now we are used to learning their spirits.
我们过去不钦佩科学家，但现在我们习惯于学习他们的精神。

重点词汇精讲

1–10
admire/ədˈmaɪə(r)/v. 钦佩；欣赏

admire sb. for sth. 因某事钦佩某人。

I admire my father for his hard work and honesty.
我钦佩我父亲的勤奋和诚实。
常考admire 后接被钦佩的人或事物，表“钦佩”而非“羡慕”。
encourage/ɪnˈkʌrɪdʒ/v. 鼓励

encourage sb. to do sth. 鼓励某人做某事；encouragement n.。

Amelia Earhart's parents always encouraged her to stick to her flying dream.
阿梅莉亚·埃尔哈特的父母总是鼓励她坚持自己的飞行梦想。
devote/dɪˈvəʊt/v. 奉献；致力于

devote oneself/one's life to doing sth. 献身于做某事，to 是介词后接动名词。

The doctor devoted all his time to helping the poor patients in mountain areas.
这位医生把全部时间都奉献给了帮助山区的贫困病人。
易错devote...to 中 to 是介词，后接 doing，不是动词原形。
courage/ˈkʌrɪdʒ/n. 勇气

have the courage to do sth. 有勇气做某事；courageous adj. 勇敢的。

His parents' support gave him the courage to keep going when he wanted to quit.
当他想要放弃时，父母的支持给了他继续前进的勇气。
wisdom/ˈwɪzdəm/n. 智慧；才智

wise adj. 明智的。

—Why is it admirable to help others? —Because it shows kindness and wisdom.
——为什么帮助他人是令人钦佩的？——因为它体现了善良和智慧。
effort/ˈefət/n. 努力；精力

make an effort to do sth. 努力做某事；spare no effort 不遗余力。

Through her efforts, Jane's dream of traveling around the world came true at last.
通过她的努力，简环游世界的梦想终于实现了。
常考make efforts / make an effort to do sth. 是高频搭配。
hero/ˈhɪərəʊ/n. 英雄

heroine n. 女英雄；复数 heroes。

The admirable hero lost his legs in the war, but he never gave up hope.
这位令人钦佩的英雄在战争中失去了双腿，但他从未放弃希望。
易错hero 复数直接加 es：heroes。
achieve/əˈtʃiːv/v. 实现；达到

achieve one's dream/goal 实现梦想/目标；achievement n. 成就。

After years of hard work, she finally achieved her dream of becoming a famous writer.
经过多年的努力，她终于实现了成为著名作家的梦想。
succeed/səkˈsiːd/v. 成功

succeed in doing sth. 成功做成某事；success n.；successful adj.；successfully adv.。

After trying many times, she finally succeeded in finishing the difficult task.
尝试了很多次之后，她终于成功完成了这项艰巨的任务。
易错succeed 后接 in doing，不接 to do。
wisely/ˈwaɪzli/adv. 明智地

wise 的副词形式。

We should use our pocket money wisely.
我们应该明智地使用零花钱。

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
It is + adj. + of / for sb. to do 辨析

of 前形容词表人的品质（kind/brave/wise/clever/nice/polite/foolish 等）；for 前形容词表事情性质（important/easy/difficult/necessary/possible 等）。

It was wise of Confucius to say that we should treat our elders with respect.
孔子说我们应该尊敬长辈，真是明智。
It is necessary for everyone to follow the traffic rules to keep safe.
每个人遵守交通规则以保证安全是必要的。
2
强调句 It was...that

强调人可用 who，其余用 that；去掉 It was...that 句子仍完整。

It was in the Eastern Han Dynasty that Cai Lun improved the paper-making technique.
正是在东汉时期，蔡伦改进了造纸术。
3
It is said/believed that 主语从句

it 作形式主语；类似结构：It is reported/known/suggested that...。

It is believed that hard work leads to success.
人们相信努力工作会带来成功。
4
定语从句 who / that / whose

先行词指人：作主语用 who/that，作宾语用 whom/that（可省略），表所属用 whose。

The man who is standing over there is my history teacher.
站在那边的那个人是我的历史老师。
She is the scientist whose discovery changed the world.
她就是那位发现改变了世界的科学家。
5
used to do / be used to doing

used to do 过去常常（现在不）；be used to doing 习惯于；be used to do 被用来。

In the past, people used to give up their dreams easily because of poor conditions.
过去，人们常常因为条件差而轻易放弃梦想。
6
现在完成时 since / for

since + 点时间/从句，for + 段时间；谓语用延续性动词。

Scientists have collected thousands of valuable seeds for our seed banks since 2001.
自2001年以来，科学家们已经为我们的种子库收集了数千颗宝贵的种子。
We have known the great seed scientist for nearly two months.
我们认识这位伟大的种子科学家已经将近两个月了。
7
yet / already 用法

yet 用于否定句与疑问句（句末），already 用于肯定句（句中/句末）。

—Have you finished reading the story about Sima Qian yet? —Yes, I have already finished it before dinner.
——你读完关于司马迁的故事了吗？——是的，我在晚饭前就已经读完了。
8
encourage / advise / ask sb. to do

动词 + sb. + to do：encourage、advise、ask、tell、want、allow 等。

Our teacher often encourages us to help each other as a team.
我们的老师经常鼓励我们作为一个团队互相帮助。
9
devote...to doing 介词 to

devote/be used to/look forward to/pay attention to 等短语中 to 为介词，后接动名词。

The old teacher devoted nearly 40 years to teaching in the mountain village.
这位老教师将近40年都奉献给了山村的教学工作。
10
名词→形容词词性转换

courage→courageous；wisdom→wise；success→successful；effort 本身为名词。

Li Na is one of the most successful tennis players in China, and she won many international prizes.
李娜是中国最成功的网球运动员之一，她赢得了许多国际奖项。
11
情景交际——征求意见

May I ask you some questions? 应答：Of course, go ahead. / Sure. / Certainly.

—Might I ask you some questions about your hero? —Of course, go ahead.
——我可以问你一些关于你英雄的问题吗？——当然，请问吧。
12
情景交际——表达赞同

I couldn't agree more 我再同意不过；That's true.；I agree with you.；Exactly.。

—I think Zhong Yang is one of the most inspiring scientists. —I couldn't agree more. His work has made a great difference to the world.
——我认为钟扬是最鼓舞人心的科学家之一。——我完全同意。他的工作对世界产生了巨大影响。

语法易错点总结

考前必看
!
姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. of sb. to do 与 for sb. to do 判断：看形容词描述人还是事，brave/kind/wise 用 of。
  2. It is said that... 中 it 是形式主语，不可省略；that 从句是真正主语。
  3. used to do（过去常常）与 be used to doing（习惯于）与 be used to do（被用来）三者易混。
  4. devote...to / look forward to / pay attention to / be used to 中 to 均为介词，后接 doing。
  5. 现在完成时 since 接点时间，for 接段时间，不可混淆。
  6. yet 用于否定与疑问，already 用于肯定；already 不与 yet 互换。
  7. succeed in doing sth. 不接 to do；success 是名词，successful 是形容词。
  8. hero 复数是 heroes，courageous 中 ous 是形容词后缀。
  9. encourage sb. to do sth. 不定式作宾语补足语，sb. 与 do 是主谓关系。
  10. 强调句中被强调部分是状语时，只能用 that 不能用 when/where。
  11. 定语从句中先行词指人作宾语时 whom/that 可省略，作主语时 who/that 不可省略。
  12. achieve 是及物动词，直接接宾语：achieve one's dream，不加介词。
