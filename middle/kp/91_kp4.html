语法核心

本单元两大语法
宾语从句（引导词全辨析）

宾语从句引导词：that（陈述）、whether/if（是否）、what/which（什么/哪个）、who（谁）、where（哪里）、when（何时）、how（如何）、why（为什么）；从句一律用陈述语序，主句过去时从句用相应过去时。

I don't know why he was late this morning.
Do you know where the library is?
remember/forget to do 与 doing

remember/forget to do sth. 记得/忘记去做（未做）；remember/forget doing sth. 记得/忘记做过（已做）。

类似结构：stop to do/stop doing；try to do/try doing；regret to do/regret doing。

Please remember to turn off the lights before you leave.
I still remember going to the beach with my parents when I was a child.
动名词作主语 · used to do · the + 比较级

动名词/不定式作主语谓语用单数：Doing more reading helps memory。

used to do sth. 过去常常做某事。

the + 比较级，the + 比较级 越……越……。

Memorizing English words every day is a good way to improve your memory.
The more you review knowledge, the better you remember it.

Wild dolphins can recognize their old partners with good memory.

A good technique is important for remembering new information.

under stress 处于压力下；stressed adj. 感到压力的。

You can write down your plans in your notebook.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
宾语从句引导词 what/which/who

what 表“什么”，which 表“哪一个（有范围）”，who 表“谁”，从句用陈述语序。

I don't understand what she's going through.
I don't know which book you like best.
2
宾语从句 where/when/how/why

where 哪里、when 何时、how 如何、why 为什么；疑问词后接陈述语序。

Could you tell me why it is easy to forget a word?
3
remember/forget to do 与 doing

to do 表示未做的事，doing 表示已做的事。

Please remember to close the windows when you leave the room.
I remember locking the door before I left home.
4
used to do sth.

过去常常做某事，现在不再做了；否定 usedn't to / didn't use to。

I used to be shy, but now I'm more outgoing.
5
动名词作主语

动名词作主语谓语用单数；也可用 it 作形式主语。

Connecting new knowledge with old knowledge is a good way to learn.
Keeping a diary is a good way to record your daily memories.
6
the + 比较级

the + 比较级，the + 比较级 表示越……越……。

The more you review what you have learned, the better grades you will get.
7
词性转换：memory/memorize

memory n. 记忆；memorize v. 记住；memorable adj. 难忘的。

Many students find it hard to memorize all the new words in a short time.
8
词性转换：confident/confidence

confident adj. 自信的；confidence n. 信心；be confident in 对……有信心。

You should be confident in yourself when you give a speech.
9
词性转换：stress/stressed

stress n./v.；stressed 修饰人，stressful 修饰物。

He felt stressed because he always forgot things.
10
keep...in mind / divide...into / link...with

keep sth. in mind 牢记；divide A into B 把 A 分成 B；link A with B 把 A 与 B 相连。

Please keep the safety rules in mind when you are at school.
11
make use of / put...to good use

make (full/good) use of 充分利用；put sth. to good use 好好利用某物。

We should put our spare time to good use to improve our English skills.
12
情景交际——没听清

Pardon me? / I beg your pardon? 请再说一遍；应答可重复内容。

—Could you please repeat the key point of the lecture? —Pardon me. I was taking notes just now.

语法易错点总结

考前必看
!
姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. 宾语从句一律陈述语序，疑问词引导时不再倒装。
  2. remember/forget to do 未做，doing 已做；结合语境判断。
  3. used to do 表过去习惯且现在停止，不与现在完成时混淆。
  4. 动名词作主语谓语用单数：Playing sports makes...。
  5. stress 名词压力；stressed 感到压力的（人），stressful 令人有压力的（事）。
  6. memory 作“回忆”讲时可数：memories；作“记忆力”讲常用单数。
  7. keep...in mind 牢记；divide...into 把……分成；link...with 连接，介词固定不可换。
  8. run out of 主语是人（用完某物）；某物 run out 主语是物（用完了）。
  9. mix up 混淆；代词作宾语放中间：mix them up。
  10. the + 比较级结构中 the 不可省略。
  11. pay attention to 中 to 是介词，后接 doing/名词。
  12. Pardon me? 是礼貌请求重复，回答时重复原内容即可。
