语法核心 重点词汇 高频短语 12 个考点

语法核心

本单元两大语法
Why 与 Because 问答

Why 询问原因，用 Because... 回答；Because 引导原因状语从句。

—Why do you like English? —Because I think it's very useful.
—Why don't you like math? —Because it's difficult.
时间介词 at / on / in

at + 具体时刻（at 8 o'clock）· on + 星期/具体日期（on Monday, on June 1st）· in + 月份/季节/年/上午下午晚上（in May, in the morning）。

We have an art lesson on Friday. It's my favorite.
—What time does your mother go to work? —At 7:30 in the morning.
学科评价形容词

interesting 有趣的 · boring 无聊的 · difficult 困难的 · easy 容易的 · useful 有用的 · fun 有趣的。

I think maths is difficult but interesting.
—Why do you like history? —Because it's interesting.

重点词汇精讲

1–10
subject/ˈsʌbdʒɪkt/n. 学科；科目

school subjects 学校科目。

—What's your favorite subject? —Math. It's interesting.
常考favourite subject 最喜欢的科目是高频考点。
maths/mæθs/n. 数学

英式拼写，美式 math；作主语谓语用单数。

—What is your favourite subject? —Maths.
易错maths 形式复数意义单数，谓语用 is。
history/ˈhɪstri/n. 历史

history book 历史书。

—Why do you like history? —Because I can learn about old stories.
biology/baɪˈɒlədʒi/n. 生物学

bio（生命）+ logy（学科）。

Biology is hard but my teacher makes it interesting.
geography/dʒiˈɒɡrəfi/n. 地理（学）

geo（地球）+ graphy。

—Why do you like geography best? —Because I can learn a lot about different countries.
favourite/ˈfeɪvərɪt/adj. 最喜欢的 · n. 最爱

无比较级、无最高级。

—What's your favorite color? —Blue.
易错favourite 本身就是“最喜欢的”，不加 most。
because/bɪˈkɒz/conj. 因为

回答 Why；because of + 名词。

He likes music because it's relaxing.
difficult/ˈdɪfɪkəlt/adj. 困难的

= hard。

I don't like math because it's difficult for me.
useful/ˈjuːsfl/adj. 有用的

use + ful；usefully 副词。

English is a useful subject.
interesting/ˈɪntrəstɪŋ/adj. 有趣的

修饰物；interested 修饰人（be interested in）。

—What do you think of the English class? —It's very interesting.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
考点一：Why 问原因

Why...? 用 Because... 回答；问句用 why，答句用 because。

—Why do you like history? —Because it's interesting.
2
考点二：because 从句位置

because 引导原因状语从句，可放主句前（加逗号）或主句后。

I have no time to play tennis with you because I am very busy today.
3
考点三：favourite 的用法

favourite 本身含“最”义，无比较级最高级；What's your favourite subject? 是最爱学科问答。

—What is his favorite subject? —His favorite subject is art.
4
考点四：时间介词 on 表星期

具体星期前用 on：on Monday / on Friday morning。

—When do you have music? —On Monday and Friday.
5
考点五：时间介词 at 表时刻

具体时刻前用 at：at 7:30 / at noon。

Ann has English at 9 o'clock.
6
考点六：学科词首字母大小写

学科名词作普通名词时小写：maths, history；课程专有名或句首大写。

My mother teaches maths in a middle school.
7
考点七：maths 作主语谓语用单数

maths 形式复数意义单数，谓语动词用单数 is。

My parents say math is very useful.
8
考点八：interesting 与 interested

interesting 修饰物（令人感兴趣的）；interested 修饰人（be interested in 对……感兴趣）。

The story is interesting, and I'm interested in it.
9
考点九：difficult / easy / useful 评价词

difficult = hard 困难的；easy 容易的；useful 有用的。

Science is difficult but interesting.
10
考点十：What about...?

What about...? = How about...? 用于建议或询问意见，后接名词或动名词。

—I think science is very interesting. What about you? —I don't think so. I like geography better.
11
考点十一：一般现在时三单复习

like→likes、teach→teaches、study→studies；主语三单时动词变化。

My favorite teacher is Miss Li. She teaches us English.
12
考点十二：情景交际——表达喜好

I like... best. / My favourite... is... 表达最喜欢；What about you? 反问对方。

—Why do you like geography best? —Because I can learn a lot about different countries.

语法易错点总结

考前必看
!
姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. Why 问原因，用 Because 回答；because 后接句子。
  2. favourite 已含“最”义，不加 most。
  3. maths 作主语谓语用单数：Maths is...。
  4. 星期前用 on：on Monday；时刻前用 at：at 8:00。
  5. interesting 修饰物，interested 修饰人（be interested in）。
  6. difficult = hard；easy 与 difficult 相对。
  7. What about...? 后接名词或动名词。
  8. I like... best. 与 My favourite... is... 都表“最喜欢”。
  9. teach sb. sth.：She teaches us English.。
  10. Because 从句在主句后不加逗号，在主句前要加逗号。
  11. have English class 上英语课，class 前可加 a/the。
  12. Why not...? 表建议：Why not try it?。
