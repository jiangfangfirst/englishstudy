八年级上册 · KP 2

要点：① 频度副词 always/usually/often/sometimes/hardly ever/never 的位置与排序；② How often 提问频率；③ could 委婉请求许可；④ borrow 与 lend 区别。
频度副词always · usually · often · never
请求许可Could I...? · Could you please...?
家庭生活housework · borrow · lend · tidy
本 KP 三个关键：① 频度副词放在实义动词前、be 动词后；② borrow 借入（from），lend 借出（to）；③ hardly ever 本身含否定，反意疑问句用肯定。

语法核心

本单元四大语法
语法一：频度副词与 How often

常见频度副词：always > usually > often > sometimes > hardly ever > never。放在实义动词之前，be 动词/助动词/情态动词之后。How often 提问频率，答语用 once a week、twice a month、every day 等。

My mother always gets up early. She is very hard-working.
Millie gets up early and she is never late for school.
—How often does your father exercise? —Twice a day.
语法二：情态动词 could 表示请求许可

Could I...? 委婉请求许可；Could you please...? 委婉请对方做事。肯定回答 Sure/Of course/No problem；否定回答 Sorry, I can't.。

—Bill, can I use your ruler? —Of course! Here you are.
—Could I use your computer for a while? —Sorry, I'm afraid not. I need it to look up some information.
语法三：一般现在时与 there be

一般现在时表经常性动作；第三人称单数谓语加 -s/-es。there be 表"某地有某物"，be 与最近的主语一致（就近原则）。

My father reads the newspaper every morning.
There is a pen and two books on the desk.
There isn't much furniture in my new bedroom, just a bed and a desk.
语法四：复合不定代词

肯定句用 some-，否定疑问用 any-；形容词修饰时后置；作主语谓语用单数。

—I want to tell you something important.
—What's the matter with you? —I have a stomachache. I can't eat anything.
—Don't play computer games again. It brings nothing useful to you. —Sorry, mom. I won't.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
考点一：How often 提问频率

How often 提问频率；How long 问时长；How soon 问多久以后；How far 问距离。

—How often do you go shopping online? —Twice a week.
2
考点二：频度副词的位置

实义动词之前，be 动词/助动词/情态动词之后。

My sister is a book lover. She always reads before going to bed.
He is the first to get to school every day. He is never late for school.
3
考点三：hardly ever 与 never

hardly ever 几乎从不，本身含否定，反意疑问句用肯定；never 从不。

The girl never goes to bed late because she has a good living habit.
4
考点四：could 表示请求许可

Could I...? 请求许可；Could you please...? 请对方做。肯定 Sure/Of course，否定 Sorry, I can't.

—Can I borrow your bike? —With pleasure.
5
考点五：borrow 与 lend

borrow 借入（from sb.）；lend 借出（to sb.）。

—May I borrow your dictionary, Lucy? —Of course, but you can only keep it for one week.
—Could I borrow your pen? —Sorry, I can't lend it to you. I'm using it now.
6
考点六：复合不定代词

肯定句用 some-，否定疑问用 any-；形容词后置；作主语谓语用单数。

—I want to tell you something important.
7
考点七：反身代词

与主语一致，常考 enjoy oneself、teach oneself、by oneself。

He is old enough to look after himself.
8
考点八：主谓一致

the number of 作主语谓语用单数；a number of 用复数；百分数作主语与 of 后名词一致。

The number of the students in our school is about 1,000.
About 76 percent of the earth is water.
9
考点九：复合形容词作定语

数词+名词+形容词构成复合形容词，名词用单数，加连字符。

The five-year-old boy can play the piano very well.
10
考点十：although 与 until

although 尽管，不与 but 连用；not...until... 直到……才……

Although I was ill, I still went to school.
—Can I stay out until ten tonight? —No, you can't. You have to come back before 9:30.
11
考点十一：spend 与 take

spend 主语是人，spend time doing；take 用 It takes sb. time to do。

The journey back to our hometown took almost six hours, but the moment we saw Grandfather waiting at the village entrance, we all felt so glad.
—How long did it take you to clean the whole apartment? —About two hours. We all worked together.
12
考点十二：情景交际

问频率用 How often；问家庭用 What...look like?；感叹用 What a sweet home!；请求用 Could I...?

My grandparents own a two-story house with a vegetable garden in the countryside.
