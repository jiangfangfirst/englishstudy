语法核心

本单元两大语法
there be 句型

there be 表示“某处有某物”，be 动词由离它最近的主语决定（就近原则）：There is a book and two pens. / There are two pens and a book.

否定：There isn't / aren't...；疑问：Is / Are there...? 回答 Yes, there is/are. / No, there isn't/aren't.

There is a library and two gyms in our school.
—Are there any computers in your classroom? —Yes. There is a computer on the teacher's desk.
方位介词

next to 紧邻 · across from 在……对面 · in front of 在……前面 · behind 在……后面 · between...and... 在……和……之间 · on the left/right of 在……的左边/右边。

The library is across from the teaching building.
The library is between the classroom building and the gym.
冠词 a / an / the

a/an 表泛指（an 用于元音音素前：an office）；the 表特指。

There is a computer and some books on the teacher's desk.
The map is on the wall.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
考点一：there be 就近原则

be 动词与最近的主语保持一致：There is a book and two pens.；There are two pens and a book.

There is a computer and some books on the teacher's desk.
2
考点二：there be 不与 have 连用

there be 表示“存在”，have 表示“拥有”，不能同句连用。

There is a library and two classroom buildings in our school.
3
考点三：there be 的否定与疑问

否定 There isn't/aren't...；疑问 Is/Are there...? 回答 Yes, there is/are. / No, there isn't/aren't.

—Is there a library in your school? —Yes, there is
4
考点四：some 与 any

肯定句用 some，否定句和疑问句用 any：There are some books. / Are there any books?

There aren't any eggs in the fridge. I need to buy some.
5
考点五：in front of 与 in the front of

in front of 在（物体外部）前面；in the front of 在（物体内部）前部。

There is a big tree in front of the classroom building.
The blackboard is in the front of the classroom.
6
考点六：next to / across from / between

next to 紧邻；across from 对面；between...and... 在两者之间。

The teachers' office is next to the art room.
The dining hall is across from the gym. We can see it when we stand at the gate of the gym.
7
考点七：介词 on / in / at 基础

on 在……上（表面）；in 在……里；at 在具体地点。

The map is on the wall.
The students are having a party in the classroom. There is some juice and a lot of snacks on each desk.
8
考点八：冠词 a / an

元音音素前用 an：an office / an art room；辅音音素前用 a：a library。

This is an office and the office is not big.
9
考点九：there be 与 it is 区分

there be 表存在；it is 表特指某物。

There is a computer and some books on the teacher's desk.
—Where is the art room? —It's on the second floor.
10
考点十：指路情景交际

Excuse me, where is...? 询问地点；Go straight and turn left/right. 指路。

—Where is the library? —It's on the second floor.
11
考点十一：询问数量

How many + 复数名词 + are there...? 询问数量；回答 There are...。

—How many classrooms are there in your school? —Ten.
12
考点十二：描述位置的主谓一致

There be 句型的疑问与否定中 be 同样遵循就近原则。

—Are there any computers in your classroom? —Yes. There is a computer on the teacher's desk.

语法易错点总结

姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. there be 遵循就近原则：is 还是 are 看最近的主语。
  2. there be 表示“存在”，不能与 have 连用。
  3. 疑问句和否定句用 any，肯定句用 some。
  4. in front of（外部前方）≠ in the front of（内部前部）。
  5. an office / an art room（元音音素开头用 an）。
  6. how many 后接复数名词：How many classrooms...?。
  7. a map of China 是“中国地图”，of 表所属。
  8. 指路时 Excuse me 用于礼貌打扰。
  9. There is... 回答 Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.。
  10. between 后接 and 连接两个对象。
  11. on the wall 表在墙面上，in the wall 表在墙里面。
  12. teachers' office 中复数所有格只加 '。
