语法核心

本单元两大语法
语法一：if 引导的条件状语从句

if 表"如果"，主句用一般将来时，从句用一般现在时（主将从现）。unless = if...not（除非）；as long as 只要。

祈使句 + and + 陈述句 表"那么就会"；祈使句 + or + 陈述句 表"否则就会"。

If you take this exam seriously enough, you will get good grades for sure.
You won't fully experience the culture of a foreign country unless you go there in person.
—Do everything carefully, or you may make mistakes. —I see. I know what to do now.
语法二：宾语从句中的 if

if 引导宾语从句表"是否"，时态根据主句变化，不遵循主将从现。与条件状语从句区分：条件句 if = 如果（主将从现）；宾从 if = 是否。

If you don't try, you will never know if you can succeed.
She will be happy if she receives the present.

重点词汇精讲

1–20
communicate/kəˈmjuːnɪkeɪt/v. 交流；沟通

搭配 communicate with sb. 与某人交流。

Good communication improves understanding between people. We should try our best to communicate well.
medium/ˈmiːdiəm/n. 媒介；手段

复数 media。

In modern society, people often use social media as a tool for communication.
offer/ˈɒfə(r)/v. 主动提出；提供

搭配 offer to do sth.；offer sth. to sb.。

He offered to help me carry the heavy box.
provide/prəˈvaɪd/v. 提供；供应

搭配 provide sb. with sth. 或 provide sth. for sb.。

The Browns often offer food to homeless people and provide books for the children in need.
trust/trʌst/v. 信任；信赖
—Why do you always believe in him? —Because I trust him completely.
calm/kɑːm/v. 使平静 · adj. 平静的

搭配 calm sb. down 使某人平静下来。

Try to keep calm and just tell me what happened.
continue/kənˈtɪnjuː/v. 继续

搭配 continue doing sth. 继续做同一件事。

After a short rest, they continued doing the work.
pay attention to/peɪ əˈtenʃn tuː/phr. 注意

to 是介词，后接名词/代词/动名词。

We should pay attention to the traffic rules when we cross the road.
argue/ˈɑːɡjuː/v. 争论；争吵

搭配 argue with sb. about sth. 与某人争论某事。

My parents often argue with me about my study habits.
impolite/ˌɪmpəˈlaɪt/adj. 不礼貌的
It's impolite to ask others personal questions in public.
face-to-face/ˌfeɪs tə ˈfeɪs/adj. 面对面的
We should have a face-to-face talk to solve the problem.
chance/tʃɑːns/n. 机会；可能性

搭配 have a chance of doing sth.。

You have a good chance of passing the exam if you are more careful.
importance/ɪmˈpɔːtns/n. 重要性
One of the most important ways to learn is to practice more.
point/pɔɪnt/v. 指 · n. 要点

搭配 point at 指向。

—There is no point talking about such a foolish thing over and over again. —I can't agree more. Let's do something meaningful.
surely/ˈʃʊəli/adv. 当然；肯定
If you practice speaking English every day, you will surely make progress.
carefully/ˈkeəfəli/adv. 仔细地；小心地
The more carefully you do your homework, the fewer mistakes you will make.
cost/kɒst/v. 花费（金钱）

主语是物。

The new bicycle cost me 200 dollars.
pay for/peɪ fɔː(r)/phr. 为……付款

主语是人。

—How much is the ticket to Inner Mongolia? —A one-way ticket costs 1800 yuan, and you can pay another 300 yuan for a round-trip.
realize/ˈriːəlaɪz/v. 意识到；实现
After reading the story of Benjamin Franklin, he finds out that lifelong learning is important.
relationship/rɪˈleɪʃnʃɪp/n. 关系

搭配 build good relationships with sb.。

Good communication can help us build good relationships with people around us.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–11
1
考点一：if 条件句主将从现

主句将来，从句一般现在时表将来。

If you want to get good grades, you should study hard.
2
考点二：if 宾从 vs 条件句

宾从 if = 是否（时态随主句）；条件句 if = 如果（主将从现）。

—Did Tina finish her job? —I don't know. But I think she will give me a call if she finishes her job.
3
考点三：unless = if...not

除非；如果不。

You won't be late for school if you get up early.
4
考点四：as long as 只要

引导条件状语从句。

I can lend my bike to you if you can take good care of it.
5
考点五：祈使句 + and/or

and 表"那么就会"；or 表"否则就会"。

Talk to your teacher and you will get help if you have a problem.
6
考点六：offer 与 provide

offer sth. to sb.；provide sb. with sth. / provide sth. for sb.

AI technology has won high praise because it can offer more opportunities to people.
7
考点七：pay for 与 cost

pay for 主语是人；cost 主语是物。

—Does the computer cost you lots of money? —Yes. I spend more than 9,000 yuan on it.
—How much is the ticket to Inner Mongolia? —A one-way ticket costs 1800 yuan, and you can pay another 300 yuan for a round-trip.
8
考点八：calm down 与 stay calm

calm sb. down 使平静；stay calm 保持冷静。

—Do you think Andy can be a good policeman? —Yes, I think so. He is able to stay calm when something dangerous happens.
9
考点九：pay attention to

to 是介词，后接名词/动名词。

We should pay more attention to our pronunciation.
10
考点十：argue with sb. about sth.

与某人争论某事。

—I argued with my best friend. What should I do? —You should call him so that you can say sorry.
11
考点十一：情景交际

回答感谢 You're welcome.；回答道歉 It doesn't matter.；安慰 Don't worry. / Stay calm.；同意 Good idea.；不同意 I'm afraid I disagree.

—Don't worry about your English. I can help you with it. —Thank you very much. It's very kind of you.

姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. if 条件句：主句将来，从句现在。
  2. if 宾从表"是否"，不要与条件句混淆。
  3. unless = if...not。
  4. as long as 只要。
  5. 祈使句 + and/or + 陈述句。
  6. offer sth. to sb. 与 provide sb. with sth. 搭配不同。
  7. pay for 主语人，cost 主语物。
  8. calm sb. down 使平静；stay calm 保持冷静。
  9. pay attention to 的 to 是介词。
  10. argue with sb. about sth. 固定搭配。
  11. 回答感谢 You're welcome.；回答道歉 It doesn't matter.
  12. 同意 Good idea.；不同意 I'm afraid I disagree.
  13. 安慰 Don't worry. / Stay calm.
  14. communicate with / offer to do / continue doing 固定搭配。
