初中英语 · 学霸笔记

八年级上册 · KP 4

要点：① 最高级构成（-est / most / 不规则）；② one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词；③ 序数词 + 最高级；④ 副词最高级 the 可省略；⑤ 否定词 + 比较级表最高级含义。
最高级the tallest · the best · the most
最高级句型one of the... · the second longest
动植物与环保bamboo · blue whale · protect · in danger
本 KP 三个关键：① 三者以上用最高级，the + -est/most；② one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词；③ 副词最高级前 the 可省。

语法核心

本单元两大语法
语法一：形容词和副词的最高级

三者或以上比较用最高级。构成：单音节加 -est（tall→tallest）；e 结尾加 -st（nice→nicest）；辅+y 变 i+est（happy→happiest）；重读闭音节双写末辅音（big→biggest）；多音节加 most（outgoing→most outgoing）；不规则：good/well→best，bad/badly→worst，many/much→most，little→least。

句型：the + 最高级 + in/of（在……中最……）；one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词（最……之一）；序数词 + 最高级（第几最……）；Which/Who is + the 最高级, A, B or C?（三者选择）。副词最高级修饰动词时 the 可省略。

The blue whale is the largest animal on the earth.
I went to Dali last week. It's one of the most beautiful places I have ever seen.
—Which is longer, the Yangtze River or the Yellow River? —The Yangtze River, of course. It is the longest river in China.
Who runs the fastest, Jim, Tim or Julie?
语法二：比较级与同级比较（复习）

两者比较用比较级 + than；much/a little/even/far 修饰比较级；比较级+and+比较级（越来越……）；the+比较级，the+比较级（越……越……）。否定词 + 比较级表最高级含义（It couldn't be better. 再好不过了）。

as + 原级 + as 和……一样；not as/so + 原级 + as 不如……。比较对象要一致，用 that/those 替代前面名词。

The harder you study, the better results you will get.
—Did you see the TV series called She and Her Girls last night? —Yes, it's so great. I have never seen a better one.
The weather in Beijing is colder than that in Guangzhou.

cheetah/ˈtʃiːtə/n. 猎豹

ginkgo/ˈɡɪŋkəʊ/n. 银杏

redwood/ˈredwʊd/n. 红杉

pollination/ˌpɒləˈneɪʃn/n. 传粉
Many fruits can't grow well without bees' pollination.
ocean/ˈəʊʃn/n. 海洋
—Why is the blue whale important to the ocean? —Because when it dies, it becomes food for many sea animals and helps create a more lively ecosystem.
symbol/ˈsɪmbl/n. 象征

搭配 a symbol of ……的象征。

The panda is one of the symbols of China.
protect/prəˈtekt/v. 保护

搭配 protect...from... 保护……免受……

We should protect frogs because they help eat insects and keep the ecosystem balanced.
environment/ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/n. 环境
Many animals hide themselves by changing their colors to match the environment.
destroy/dɪˈstrɔɪ/v. 破坏；毁坏
We should not destroy forests to build more roads.
danger/ˈdeɪndʒə(r)/n. 危险

搭配 in danger 处于危险中。

Many animals are in danger because of the pollution.
service/ˈsɜːvɪs/n. 服务
The service in this hotel is friendly and fast.
popular/ˈpɒpjələ(r)/adj. 受欢迎的

搭配 be popular with 受……欢迎。

Playing phone games is popular with many students, but don't spend too much time on it.
serious/ˈsɪəriəs/adj. 严肃的；严重的
Air pollution is a serious problem in many cities.
warm/wɔːm/adj. 温暖的
These birds always appear in warm weather. So you can see them in early summer.
longest/ˈlɒŋɡɪst/adj. 最长的
The Yangtze River is one of the longest rivers in the world.
highest/ˈhaɪɪst/adj. 最高的
—Which mountain is the highest, Mount Tai, Mount Huang or Mount Tianzi? —Mount Tianzi, I think.
fastest-growing/ˈfɑːstɪst ˈɡrəʊɪŋ/adj. 生长最快的
Bamboo is one of the fastest-growing plants in the world.

中考常考考点与易错点

1–12
1
考点一：最高级的构成

单音节加 -est，多音节加 most；重读闭音节双写；辅+y 变 i+est。

—This dress is too expensive. Do you have a cheaper one? —Sorry, but that's the cheapest one.
2
考点二：最高级句型

the+最高级+in/of；one of the+最高级+复数名词；序数词+最高级。

—Is Jay Chou the most popular singer of all the singers? —Of course. He is my favorite.
Canada is the second largest country of the world.
3
考点三：副词最高级

副词最高级修饰动词时，the 可省略。

Jim runs faster than Tony, but Frank runs fastest of them.
4
考点四：比较级与最高级区别

两者用比较级，三者以上用最高级。

—Do you think Mike is the tallest in our class? —No. Dave is taller than him.
He is the tallest student in our class.
5
考点五：比较级 + and + 比较级

表示"越来越……"。

Our city is becoming more and more beautiful.
6
考点六：the + 比较级，the + 比较级

表示"越……越……"。

The harder you study, the better results you will get.
7
考点七：否定词 + 比较级表最高级

It couldn't be better. 再好不过了。

—Did you see the TV series called She and Her Girls last night? —Yes, it's so great. I have never seen a better one.
8
考点八：as...as 与 not as/so...as

as...as 中间用原级；not as/so...as 不如……。

China is almost as big as the US, and it is the biggest country in Asia.
9
考点九：比较对象要一致

用 that/those 代替前面提到的名词。

The weather in Beijing is much colder than in Guangzhou in winter.
10
考点十：动植物词汇辨析

bamboo、bee、blue whale、cheetah、ginkgo、redwood、pollination、ocean、symbol。

Elephants are the biggest land animals in the world.
11
考点十一：保护动物与环境

protect、in danger、take action、plant more trees、cut down。

It's important for us to care for wild animals instead of hunting them for money.
12
考点十二：情景交际

问最……用 What/Which is the 最高级...?；同意 I agree；祝贺 Congratulations。

—Which is the biggest, the sun, the moon or the earth? —The sun, of course.

语法易错点总结

考前必看
!
姜老师提醒 · 高频易错，务必牢记
  1. 三者以上用最高级，形容词最高级前通常加 the。
  2. 副词最高级前 the 可省略。
  3. 单音节加 -est，多音节加 most。
  4. 重读闭音节双写末辅音再加 -est。
  5. 辅+y 结尾变 y 为 i 再加 -est。
  6. good/well→best，bad/badly→worst。
  7. one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。
  8. 序数词 + 最高级 表"第几最……"。
  9. 两者比较用比较级，三者以上用最高级。
  10. 比较级+and+比较级 表"越来越……"。
  11. the+比较级，the+比较级 表"越……越……"。
  12. 否定词+比较级 表最高级含义。
  13. as...as 中间用原级。
  14. 比较对象一致，用 that/those 替代。
  15. 动植物词汇准确辨析。
  16. in danger / protect / destroy / take action 熟练运用。
