词性 · Parts of Speech

非谓语动词 Non-finite Verbs

to do / doing / do 三种形式 · 固定搭配 · 作主语宾语定语状语——单选与语法填空的拉分点

to do/doing/do 固定搭配 非谓语作主语宾语定语状语
01

核心考点概览

考什么 · 怎么考

非谓语动词是初中语法的「高峰区」，也是中考单项选择、语法填空、完成句子每年必考的拉分点。一个句子里已经有了谓语动词，再出现第二个动词时，它就必须变成非谓语形式——动词不定式 to do、动名词 / 现在分词 doing、动词原形 do（使役 / 感官动词后）。初中阶段不要求掌握分词作状语的全部用法，重点是「哪些动词后接 to do、哪些接 doing、哪些接 do」的固定搭配。

to do / doing / do
三种非谓语形式的基本含义：to do 表「目的 / 将要」；doing 表「主动 / 进行 / 习惯」；do 用在使役、感官动词后。
固定搭配
want to do, enjoy doing, let sb do, practice doing, ask sb to do 等必须逐个背熟。
作主语宾语定语状语
to do / doing 在句中能充当什么成分：作主语、宾语、定语、宾语补足语、目的状语。
02

考点逐一精讲

规则 · 公式 · 搭配
考点 1

to do / doing / do

一个简单句只能有一个谓语动词。当句中已经有了谓语，再出现第二个动作时，第二个动词就要变「非谓语」。初中阶段最常见的就是三种：动词不定式 to do、动名词 / 现在分词 doing、动词原形 do（使役、感官动词后作宾补）。

to do：to + 动词原形，表「目的、将来、具体某次」——I want to join the music club.
doing：动词 + -ing，表「习惯、爱好、主动、进行」——I enjoy watching English news.
do（原形）：使役动词 let / make / have + 宾语 + 动词原形；感官动词 see / hear / watch / notice + 宾语 + do（全过程）/ doing（正在进行）。
只接 to do 的动词
want, would like, hope, wish, plan, decide, expect, refuse, learn, agree, promise：I plan to watch Days of Our Past.
只接 doing 的动词
enjoy, finish, practice, mind, keep, miss, suggest, be busy, be worth：I practice playing the guitar every day.
接 do（原形）的使役 / 感官动词
let / make / have sb do；see / hear / watch / notice sb do（全过程）：Let's go to the music room.
接 sb to do 的动词
ask, tell, want, would like, invite, teach, expect, wish sb to do：My parents want me to be a doctor.
remember / forget / stop to do vs doing
remember to do 记得去做（未做）；remember doing 记得做过（已做）。stop to do 停下来去做另一件事；stop doing 停止正在做的事。（教辅例句）
like / love / hate to do vs doing
意思基本相同：like to do（某次具体）/ like doing（长期爱好）。I like to follow the story. / I like watching the English news.

考点提示：判断用 to do 还是 doing，先看它前面那个动词是谁——不同动词搭配不同，必须一个个背。

考点 2

固定搭配

固定搭配是初中非谓语最硬的考点，靠「动词 + 固定形式」决定答案。下面四组是人教版教材反复出现、中考最高频的搭配，必须背到条件反射。

动词 + to do：want / hope / plan / decide / expect / would like + to do
动词 + doing：enjoy / finish / practice / mind / keep on / be busy / give up + doing
动词 + sb (to) do：ask / tell / want / invite / expect sb to do；let / make / have sb do（不加 to）
want to do / would like to do
想要做某事：What club do you want to join? / I'd like beef noodles, please.
hope to do / plan to do / expect to do
希望 / 计划 / 期待做某事：I hope to find out what's going on around the world. / What do you plan to watch tonight?
practice doing / enjoy doing / finish doing
练习 / 喜欢 / 完成做某事：I practice playing the guitar. / I love watching them! / After doing my homework...
keep on doing / keep doing
继续 / 一直做某事：I'm going to keep on writing stories, of course.（八上 U6 教材句）
be good at doing / be interested in doing
介词 at / in 后接动名词：I'm good at telling stories.（七上 U5 教材句）
spend time (in) doing
花时间做某事：He spends lots of time fishing.（七上 U2 教材句）
ask / tell / want sb to do
让某人做某事（带 to）：My parents want me to be a doctor.
let / make / have sb do（省 to）
使役动词后宾补用原形：Let's go to the music room. / Let me help you.
Would you mind doing...?
介意做某事（后接 doing）：Would you mind opening the window?（教辅例句）
be ready to do / be the first to do
be + adj. + to do；序数词 + to do：He was always ready to try his best. / Shen Nong was the first to discover tea.

考点提示：介词（in / at / on / of / about / without）后面一律接 doing——look forward to doing、be interested in doing、be good at doing、without doing。

考点 3

非谓语作主语宾语定语状语

非谓语动词不能作谓语，但可以在句中充当其他成分。初中最常考的是四种：作主语、作宾语、作定语、作状语（以及作宾语补足语）。

作主语：To do sth is ... / It is + adj. + to do sth　例：To learn English well is important. = It is important to learn English well.
作宾语：主语 + 及物动词 + to do / doing　例：I want to join the club. / I enjoy watching news.
作定语：不定代词 / 序数词 / the first / the best + to do　例：I have a lot of homework to do. / She is the first to come.
作状语：to do 表目的（「为了…」）　例：He ran fast to catch the bus.（教辅例句）
作主语（to do / doing 均可）
Swimming is good exercise.（动名词作主语，泛指）；To see is to believe.（不定式，具体）。It 作形式主语更常见。
it 作形式主语
It's easy to have a healthy lifestyle.（教辅例句）；真正的主语是后面的 to do。
作动词宾语
接在 want / hope / plan / enjoy / finish 等动词后：I hope to be a TV reporter one day.（八上 U5 教材句）
作形容词宾语（be + adj. + to do）
I'm happy to see you. / We are ready to start.（教辅例句）
作后置定语（to do 放在名词后）
I have a lot of homework to do. / Greenwood Park is the best place to go to on weekends.（八上 U10 教材句）
作目的状语（to do = in order to do）
We need to share the housework to have a clean and comfortable home.（八下 U3 教材句，to have 表目的）
作宾语补足语
My parents want me to be a doctor. / I like to watch her play the violin.（七上 U5 教材句）
介词后接 doing（必考点）
look forward to doing, be good at doing, be interested in doing, without doing, after doing：After doing my homework, I sometimes read a book.

考点提示：to do 既能作宾语又能作定语、状语；doing 主要作宾语、介词宾语和主语。看到介词（about / at / in / of / without / after）后面空，直接填 doing。

04

易错点

错在哪 · 怎么改
易错点 1

want 后漏加 to

错误写法
I want join the music club.
正确写法
I want to join the music club.

解析：want 后面接动词时必须用不定式 to do：want to do sth。同类还有 hope / plan / decide / would like。

易错点 2

enjoy / practice 后误接 to do

错误写法
I enjoy to play the guitar.
正确写法
I enjoy playing the guitar.

解析：enjoy / finish / practice / mind / keep 后面必须接动名词 doing，不能接 to do。

易错点 3

使役动词 let / make 后误加 to

错误写法
Let us to go to the park.
正确写法
Let us go to the park.

解析：使役动词 let / make / have 后接宾语 + 动词原形（省 to），不能加 to。被动语态时 to 要还原：He was made to work 12 hours a day.（教辅例句）

易错点 4

介词后误用动词原形

错误写法
He is good at tell stories.
正确写法
He is good at telling stories.

解析：介词 at / in / on / of / about / without 后面接动词时，一律用动名词 doing。

易错点 5

两个谓语动词之间未变非谓语

错误写法
I want join the club.
正确写法
I want to join the club.

解析：一个简单句只能有一个谓语动词；want 已经是谓语，后面的 join 必须变非谓语 to do。

易错点 6

stop to do 与 stop doing 混用

错误写法
The teacher came in. The students stopped to talk.
正确写法
The teacher came in. The students stopped talking.

解析：stop doing 是「停止正在做的事」；stop to do 是「停下来去做另一件事」。老师进来，学生是「停止讲话」，用 talking。（教辅例句）

易错点 7

ask / tell 后漏 to

错误写法
My parents ask me study hard.
正确写法
My parents ask me to study hard.

解析：ask / tell / want / invite / expect sb 后面接宾补时必须带 to：sb to do sth。（教辅例句）

易错点 8

Would you like 后误加 doing

错误写法
Would you like going to the movies?
正确写法
Would you like to go to the movies?

解析：would like = want，后面接 to do sth，不能接 doing。（教辅例句）

易错点 9

make sb do 被动语态漏 to

错误写法
The boss made the workers work 12 hours. → The workers were made work 12 hours.
正确写法
The workers were made to work 12 hours.

解析：主动语态 make sb do（省 to），变成被动语态时 to 必须还原：be made to do。（教辅例句）

05

关键提醒 · 速记口诀

口诀 · 信号词
只接 to do 的动词口诀
想要希望同意答应，决定计划学会选择——want, hope, wish, agree, promise, decide, plan, learn, choose 后面都接 to do。（教辅口诀）
只接 doing 的动词口诀
考虑建议盼原谅，承认结束否认忙，避免放弃不禁想，介意练习完上场——consider, suggest, look forward to, excuse, admit, finish, deny, be busy, avoid, give up, can't help, mind, practice, enjoy 后都接 doing。（教辅口诀）
「使役感官」后省 to 的动词口诀
使役 make / let / have，感官 see / hear / watch / notice / feel，宾语后面省 to do；一变被动 to 回来。
信号词——看到这些词，后面非谓语形式就定了
want / hope / plan / decide / would like → to do enjoy / finish / practice / mind / keep / be busy → doing let / make / have / see / hear / watch sb → do ask / tell / want / invite / expect sb → to do 介词 in / at / on / of / about / without / after → doing It's + adj. + ___ → to do（真正主语后置）

小练习

做完再点开答案

A. joinB. to joinC. joining
查看答案
B。want 后接不定式 to do：want to join the club。
2.I enjoy ____ English news every night.
A. watchB. to watchC. watching
查看答案
C。enjoy 后接动名词 doing：enjoy watching sth。
3.My parents want me ____ a doctor when I grow up.
A. beB. to beC. being
查看答案
B。want sb to do sth：想让某人做某事，带 to。

查看答案

A. tellB. to tellC. telling
查看答案
C。介词 at 后面接动名词 doing：be good at doing sth。

A. beB. to beC. being

B。hope 后接不定式 to do：hope to be sth。
